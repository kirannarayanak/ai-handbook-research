### Presentation, design and document tools

These six tools form the output layer on top of general assistants and office suites: they turn text, data and documents into decks, graphics, prototypes and document summaries. Gamma and Canva compete for prompt-to-presentation and on-brand collateral (Gamma launched Gamma Imagine in March 2026 to take on Canva and Adobe, per TechCrunch). Adobe Firefly is the image/video engine marketed on commercial safety and Content Credentials. Acrobat AI Assistant is a different category (document comprehension over PDFs). Figma AI and FigJam AI are the product-design pair sharing one seat model and one AI-credit pool. Enterprises typically standardise on the suite they already license and allow one AI presentation tool under a Business/Enterprise tier where content training is contractually off. Recurring governance issues are consumption-based AI credits and default content training on lower tiers (verified for Figma and Gamma Pro; Canva and Adobe claims could not be re-opened and are marked not verified).

*Fact-check:* Gamma business and enterprise help-centre claims and Figma pricing, content-training defaults and certifications were confirmed on opened pages. Canva and Adobe vendor pages (pricing, trust, compliance, newsroom) returned 403 or errors, so most of their pricing, data-handling and compliance claims could not be verified and were downgraded to 'not verified' with low confidence. The Gamma pricing page and trust centre were not readable, so lower-tier prices, hosting and SOC 2 date details are unverified. Remaining cited news, release and review pages were only spot-checked for existence, not line by line. 8 correction(s) or removal(s) were applied.
