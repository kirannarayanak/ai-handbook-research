## Additional departments the handbook omits

18 functions common in large organisations, each with documented AI use in 2024-2026, are absent from the handbook's 60. Three research lenses were used (core corporate functions, industry and operational functions, AI-era governance functions); candidates were fact-checked and de-duplicated against the 60.

*Core corporate and shared-service functions:* The 60 departments cover governance, finance, HR, go-to-market, technology and operations well, but miss several external-facing and workplace shared services that large companies staff separately. All five candidates are absent from the 60 and none is a renamed duplicate. Executive Administration overlaps most with the Office of the CEO and Workplace Administration. Near-misses left out as not verified: internal communications, sales enablement, legal operations, knowledge management, physical security, events and localisation.

*Industry and operational functions:* The 60 departments cover corporate, commercial, technology and support functions but omit line-of-business functions: plants, laboratories, field crews, stores, claims desks and fraud operations. Checks found no candidate that duplicates an existing department, though Quality Control Laboratory Operations and Manufacturing Operations sit closest to Quality Management (58) and Supply Chain (60). All nine candidates have at least one opened page showing AI use, but most evidence is vendor, analyst or trade-press material with self-reported figures. Grid & Network Operations and Quality Control Laboratory Operations are the weakest, since each rests largely on a session abstract or a vendor article.

*AI-era governance and enablement functions:* The 60 departments cover risk, compliance, data engineering and AI/ML build work, but they have no home for the control and enablement layer that AI adoption created: the offices that approve AI use cases, validate AI models, steward the data that feeds them, and enable revenue teams to use AI. Near-misses are 5 Enterprise Risk Management, 48 Security Governance, Risk & Privacy and 51 AI, ML & Automation, which hold parts of these functions without owning them. Developer experience, customer insights, digital workplace and AI centres of excellence were judged folded into 43/44, 33, 45/18 and 51. A knowledge management function had only one opened source and was dropped.
