## Tool deep dives

Every one of the 57 tools, grouped into the ten categories used in the catalog. Each entry was researched from pages the researcher opened and then re-checked by an independent fact-checker; the confidence label is the fact-checker's. Prices are list prices as published on the date opened and change often.

### General AI assistants and research tools

These six are horizontal assistants rather than departmental systems. Gemini, Claude and ChatGPT Enterprise compete as general chat-plus-agent platforms, while NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook) and Perplexity specialise in grounded research: the former over uploaded documents, the latter over the live web with citations. Overlap is growing: the big assistants offer deep research, file creation, connectors and agents; Perplexity offers third-party models (GPT, Gemini, Claude) inside its own interface. Enterprises usually standardise on the assistant bundled with their productivity suite, add one frontier chat platform for knowledge workers, and buy Perplexity Enterprise or Gemini Notebook as a niche research tool. Key procurement differences: training defaults (enterprise tiers exclude training by default; consumer tiers of Gemini, Claude and Perplexity differ), compliance coverage (varies by tier and sub-feature) and pricing model (flat seats versus seat-plus-usage). The claim that Gemini Enterprise routes work to Claude is not verified on an opened page.

*Fact-check:* Claude, Perplexity Enterprise and Google privacy/compliance claims were largely confirmed on opened vendor pages; Claude pricing and Enterprise Max pricing matched exactly. All openai.com and help.openai.com pages returned HTTP 403, so ChatGPT Enterprise data-handling, features and dates are unverified and its confidence is lowered to low; only third-party pricing (CloudZero) was confirmed. Several Google and Perplexity price points, dated news items and third-party-tracker figures could not be confirmed and are marked 'not verified' or removed. Trust-centre pages (Anthropic, Perplexity) and notebook.google returned no text, so FedRAMP/CSA/ISO-type claims resting on them were removed or flagged. 11 correction(s) or removal(s) were applied.

**Gemini** (Google (Alphabet); medium confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Google (Alphabet). Family: Gemini app (Google AI Plus/Pro/Ultra), Gemini in Google Workspace, Gemini Enterprise (Google Cloud; Business/Standard/Plus editions).
- **What it is:** Google's general AI assistant. Surfaces: the Gemini web/mobile app; the side panel in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Chat, Drive and Vids; Chrome; and the Gemini Enterprise app on Google Cloud with connectors to non-Google systems.
- **Key capabilities:** Deep Research, Canvas, Gems, Gemini Live, image/video generation; Drafting and summarisation in Gmail/Docs; Meet note-taking; Gemini Enterprise: permission-aware search over connectors and no-code agents (connector count and multi-model routing not verified); Bundled Gemini Notebook (ex-NotebookLM) for source-grounded research
- **Enterprise data handling:** Workspace: content is not human-reviewed or used for model training outside your domain without permission; Gemini app conversation auto-delete options 3, 18 or 36 months, default 18 months. Gemini in Workspace apps and the Gemini app have FedRAMP High; SOC 1/2/3, ISO 27001/27017/27018/27701/42001 and HIPAA support per Google's privacy hub. Google states Gemini Notebook and Gemini in Chrome do NOT support ISO/SOC/FedRAMP and are not covered by the HIPAA BAA. Gemini Enterprise Standard/Plus (and Notebook Enterprise) support data residency, CMEK (US/EU multi-region), VPC-SC and Access Transparency, with some limits (e.g. not with Grounding with Google Search); the Business edition's controls are not verified. Consumer plans sit under consumer terms.
- **Licensing and pricing:** Consumer (verified on gemini.google/subscriptions): AI Plus $4.99, AI Pro $19.99, AI Ultra from $99.99 to $199.99/month. Workspace Business Standard US$14/user/month (annual) including Gemini: NOT verified (pricing page text showed no prices); Workspace page confirms Gemini Notebook is included in plans and Enterprise is contact-sales. Gemini Enterprise per-seat prices (Business ~$21, Standard/Plus ~$30) and pay-as-you-go overages: not verified on an opened page; treat as indicative only.
- **Integrations:** Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Chat, Drive, Vids, Chrome. Specific third-party connector list (Microsoft 365, Slack, Salesforce, etc.) and the Workspace MCP server: not verified.
- **2025-2026 developments:** 16 Jul 2026: NotebookLM renamed Gemini Notebook (verified). Gemini 4 Argon is available to AI Pro/Ultra (verified on subscriptions page). Other items (Cloud Next Apr 2026 announcements, Aug 2026 pay-as-you-go edition, 8 Oct 2026 Gemini agent): not re-verified here.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** Strongest where Workspace is the system of record: Core 01, 04, 11, 13, 18, 28, 29, 31, 33, 39, 54 and analytics-heavy 16, 20, 50 via Sheets. Specialist fits (06-08, 23, 47-49, 51, 53) are weak: a general assistant without domain tooling.
- **Training notes:** Master @-mentioning Drive files, Gems, Deep Research and citation checking, and understand how sharing permissions and DLP bound what Gemini can see; admins must choose edition and set retention. Core 6-8 h; Advanced 12-16 h; Specialist 20-30 h.
- **Caveats:** Overlapping products under one brand with different terms; features vary by edition and region; Notebook/Chrome compliance gap; personal Google accounts fall under consumer terms; enterprise prices unverified; outputs need verification.

Consumer Free/Pro/Max follow a separate consumer policy (not re-checked beyond the pointer on the commercial page). Certifications listed by Anthropic: HIPAA-ready configuration (BAA available), ISO 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 42001:2023, SOC 2 Type I & II. FedRAMP High/DoD IL4-5, CSA STAR, CMEK, SCIM, audit logs and Compliance API: not verified (Trust Center page returned no text).
- **Licensing and pricing:** USD per pricing page: Free $0; Pro $17/month annual ($200 up front) or $20 monthly; Max from $100 (5x and 20x tiers, $100/$200). Team (2 to 150 seats): Standard $20/seat/month annual or $25 monthly; Premium $100 annual or $125 monthly. Enterprise: US$20/seat/month billed annually, 20-seat minimum, plus usage at API rates.
- **Integrations:** Microsoft 365 (incl. Outlook), Chrome, connectors and MCP; API via Anthropic. Availability on AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry and a list of named apps (Salesforce, Figma, Canva etc.): not verified.
- **2025-2026 developments:** Pricing page: Claude Cowork is now just Claude; Claude Dashboards (beta), Claude Science, Claude Duet and Claude Tag appear in the product menu. Detailed model release timeline, retirement dates, revenue figure and Oct 2026 items from the earlier draft: not verified.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** Strong for long-document analysis and drafting: 02, 03, 05-08, 20, 21, 27, 39, 53 (Advanced/Specialist) plus Core 01, 04, 11, 13, 31, 33, 34. Weak: 37 Customer Support, 45 IT, 46 ERP, 47 Cyber Ops and 60 Supply Chain, where it is a drafting aid only.
- **Training notes:** Prompting with Projects, connector scopes, verifying citations and numbers, artifact/file workflows and skills; admins learn retention and feedback settings; technical roles add Claude Code. Core 6-10 h; Advanced 12-18 h; Specialist 24-40 h.
- **Caveats:** Enterprise usage is billed at API rates on top of seats; 20-seat annual minimum; consumer training defaults differ, so block personal accounts; HIPAA only via BAA-configured Enterprise/API; frequent model deprecations; Trust Center documents are gated.

**NotebookLM** (Google; medium confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Google. Rename: NotebookLM became Gemini Notebook on 16 Jul 2026 (verified); NotebookLM Enterprise is now Gemini Notebook Enterprise (Google Cloud docs).
- **What it is:** Source-grounded research and writing tool: users upload documents and sources and receive cited answers plus generated study and briefing artefacts. Enterprise edition runs inside a Google Cloud project.
- **Key capabilities:** Cited Q&A over uploaded sources; Audio and Video Overviews, mind maps, reports, quizzes and similar outputs (per Google subscription pages; full list not verified); Per-notebook source and query limits vary by plan (support page shows tiers of 100/300/500 per notebook); the earlier 'free 50 sources' figure is not verified
- **Enterprise data handling:** Consumer: content not used to directly train foundation models unless the user gives feedback; feedback is human-reviewed and retained up to 3 years, disconnected from the account. Workspace/Education: uploads, queries and responses are not human-reviewed (even with feedback) and not used to train models. Google states Gemini Notebook does not support ISO/SOC/FedRAMP and is not covered by the HIPAA BAA. Gemini Notebook Enterprise supports data residency, CMEK (US/EU multi-region), VPC-SC and Access Transparency per Google Cloud docs.
- **Licensing and pricing:** Free with a Google account; higher limits via Google AI Plus $4.99, Pro $19.99, Ultra $99.99-$199.99/month (verified). Included in Workspace plans (Workspace pricing page); the $14 Business Standard price is not verified. Enterprise edition pricing: not verified; contact Google.
- **Integrations:** Google Drive, Docs and Slides, URLs; Office file support and export formats: not verified.
- **2025-2026 developments:** 16 Jul 2026 rename to Gemini Notebook, with automatic redirects (verified). Other dated items (8 Jun, 1 Aug, 14 Sep, 22 Oct 2026): not re-verified.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 13 L&D (Core); 33 Market Research, 04 Board Secretariat and 39 Product Management for document search. Specialist 03, 06, 07 only on Gemini Notebook Enterprise because the consumer/Workspace edition lacks attestations.
- **Training notes:** Curating and trusting sources, reading citations, sharing scopes, choosing the right edition, reviewing generated overviews for oversimplification. Core 6-8 h; Specialist 20 h including governance set-up.
- **Caveats:** Rename leaves mixed documentation; compliance gap outside the Enterprise edition; per-notebook limits; Enterprise pricing unpublished.

Files attached to sessions are deleted after 7 days; custom session retention needs 50+ Pro seats or one Max seat. SOC 2 Type II; Perplexity states SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR and PCI DSS (Enterprise Max help article). ISO 27001 and FedRAMP 20x Low: not verified. Data-residency options: none found.
