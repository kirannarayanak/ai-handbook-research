### Operations, PMO and transformation

Research for this cluster is incomplete. Only two vendor pages (Atlassian Jira AI and UiPath Autopilot) were opened and both were re-opened and checked in verification. Other attempts failed (PMI returned 403; ServiceNow and McKinsey pages failed; the Microsoft and SAP pages held no usable product content). Every department therefore has at most one opened source, below the two-source target, and all are low confidence. Statements marked "not verified" come from general practice, not from an opened page. The expected shared pattern is that all seven departments turn semi-structured operational records (tickets, status updates, SOPs, audit findings, incident logs) into summaries, drafts and exception lists. The shared risks are confidential project, supplier and incident data, personal data in tickets and change feedback (GDPR), and unreviewed AI output entering formal records (SLAs, quality records, recovery plans). Suggested joint training is a shared module on process and ticket data hygiene and human review of AI outputs. Joint sessions are also suggested for 55/56/58 (process and SOP work), 54/57 (portfolio and change reporting), and 59 with 55 (incident communications). All of this needs verification before it goes into the handbook. A follow-up with fresh search budget is recommended.

*Fact-check:* Both opened sources (Atlassian Jira AI, UiPath Autopilot) exist and support the product capabilities attributed to them, including the 50% faster delivery vendor claim, the Work Readiness Checker, status drafting, BPMN capture and queue monitoring. A few phrasings overreached or were wrongly attributed and were corrected. The remaining content is mostly general-practice reasoning marked "not verified", and five departments have no source that supports their core use cases. Treat the cluster as a low-confidence draft; only Jira (54) and UiPath (56) tool claims are source-backed. 6 correction(s) or removal(s) were applied.

**54 Project & Program Management Office** (Core; medium confidence)

- **AI use cases in practice:**
    - Drafting project status updates from Rovo chat in Jira (Rovo Project Management skills: create work items, update work items, draft status updates). [1]
    - Summarising a Jira work item's status, contributors, next steps, blockers and recent updates for fast catch-up. [1]
    - Breaking work down into manageable tasks with summaries and descriptions via Rovo's work breakdown. [1]
    - Checking that work is clear and complete before planning via a 'Work Readiness Checker' agent, which Atlassian says reduces wasted work and follow-up meetings (vendor claim). [1]
    - Dependency and risk summaries from exported plans using a general LLM: not verified.
- **Capstone deliverable:** A weekly portfolio status pack for a real or realistic 3-project portfolio. It contains an AI-drafted status report per project, a dependency and risk summary, and a one-page executive view. Each project's figures are validated against the source tracker. The deliverable includes a documented prompt library and a measured time-saved log (baseline versus AI-assisted). Core-tier hours.
- **Data risks and rules:** Project plans, budgets, vendor contracts and unannounced strategic initiatives are confidential. Resource data (names, utilisation) is personal data under GDPR and employment law. Rules: use only the enterprise-licensed assistant, exclude M&A or other MNPI-bearing projects from non-approved tools, and have a human owner verify every figure before it reaches a steering committee. The Atlassian page states privacy-by-design and that organisation admins can deactivate AI [1]; contractual data terms are not verified.
- **Adoption KPIs:** Percentage of status reports AI-assisted; hours to produce the weekly report (baseline versus now); report factual-error rate found on review; share of PMs trained and active weekly; reduction in status meetings or follow-up queries.
- **Curriculum emphasis:** Mostly hands-on: status and risk summarisation, prompt templates, verification against tracker data. Prioritise Jira Atlassian Intelligence (in-system data; it is where status data lives), Microsoft 365 Copilot (meeting and email context), and Claude or Gemini (long-document synthesis). Defer Gamma and Power BI Copilot to a short module.
- **Tool-list fit:** The list is a good fit. Jira's AI is the natural core, and the Atlassian page shows status drafting and summaries [1]. Gamma is useful for decks but secondary. The Atlassian page brands these features as Rovo, which the handbook's name 'Jira Atlassian Intelligence' may not reflect [1]; other PPM tools such as Asana, monday.com or MS Project/Planner AI are not verified and not sourced.
- **Sources (1):** [1] [Jira AI (Atlassian Rovo in Jira)](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/ai)
