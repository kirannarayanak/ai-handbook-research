### Security, cloud operations and observability AI

These seven tools are embedded, platform-native assistants and agents rather than general chat tools, and each is only as good as the telemetry it sits on. Microsoft Security Copilot, CrowdStrike Charlotte AI and Google Gemini in Security compete directly as SOC triage and investigation agents (departments 45/47). Microsoft Purview is the data-governance counterpart (department 48), and it also governs Copilot and other AI use. Azure Copilot, Datadog Bits AI and Dynatrace Intelligence (formerly Davis AI) address cloud and SRE operations (departments 43/44), with Datadog and Dynatrace competing as observability platforms. Enterprises usually standardise on the AI of the platform they already run, such as Microsoft 365 E5 and Azure, Falcon, Google SecOps, Datadog or Dynatrace, rather than buying across vendors. In all seven the pricing model is consumption or bundle based (SCUs, credits, commitments, ingestion), and the dominant 2025-26 trend is a move from chat assistants to semi-autonomous agents that need human-approval guardrails.

*Fact-check:* Pricing, data-handling and recent-development claims were checked against opened vendor pages and most matched (Security Copilot SCU rates and E5/E7 inclusion, Charlotte 50 free credits and ISO 42001, Datadog credit pricing and GA, Dynatrace host rates and EU/US routing, Azure Copilot cost and agent status, Purview Singapore prices). Several secondary claims could not be confirmed and were removed or marked not verified, notably the Google 5-million-alerts figure, CrowdStrike pricing-page tier figures, and Gemini package inclusion. Product-page URLs for Security Copilot, Google Security AI, Azure Copilot and Purview were dropped from sources where they were not opened or did not support the attributed claims. Vendor-reported benchmarks remain vendor claims, so confidence is medium wherever attestations or AI-specific pricing are unverified. 8 correction(s) or removal(s) were applied.

**Microsoft Security Copilot** (Microsoft (Microsoft Security product family: Defender, Entra, Intu...; high confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Microsoft (Microsoft Security product family: Defender, Entra, Intune, Purview, Sentinel)
- **What it is:** A generative-AI security assistant and agent platform. It works in a standalone portal and embedded inside Defender, Entra, Intune and Purview. It summarises incidents, guides investigations and runs autonomous agents such as Phishing Triage and Conditional Access Optimization.
- **Key capabilities:** Incident and alert summarisation with step-by-step response guidance; Embedded agents in Defender, Entra, Intune and Purview (phishing triage, vulnerability remediation, alert triage); Promptbooks and a standalone natural-language investigation workspace; Partner-built and community-built (no-code) agents; Cross-product context spanning identities, devices, data, clouds and apps
- **Enterprise data handling:** Applies to the commercial service. Customer Data is stored in the Workspace geo chosen at onboarding, which cannot be changed afterwards. Docs state data is not shared with OpenAI or used to train the Azure OpenAI foundation model. A separate data-sharing setting is ON by default for customers without an M365 E5/E7 licence (E5/E7 customers have different defaults per the Learn page). If opted in, prompts and responses go to Microsoft for product improvement and may be stored outside the chosen geo, and uploaded-file content may be human-reviewed. Admins (Global Admin, Security Admin with Capacity Contributor) can change it. Customer Data is deleted within 180 days of removing all provisioned capacity, and session data after 180 days of inactivity. Agent emails or Teams messages can cross geographic boundaries in transit. SOC 2, ISO 27001 and HIPAA attestations were not verified on opened pages.
- **Licensing and pricing:** Standalone: provisioned capacity in Security Compute Units (SCUs). The worked example on the pricing page uses USD 4 per provisioned SCU per hour (billed monthly) and USD 6 per overage SCU, minimum 1 SCU. Included at no added cost for eligible Microsoft 365 E5 and E7 customers: 400 SCUs per month per 1,000 paid user licences, capped at 10,000 SCUs per month, on a phased rollout that began 18 Nov 2025.
- **Integrations:** Microsoft Defender, Entra, Intune, Purview and Sentinel; partner-built agents. ServiceNow-style ticketing integration was not verified. Relevant to departments 45, 47 and 48 through embedded Purview and Entra agents.
- **2025-2026 developments:** Nov 2025 (Ignite): Security Copilot bundled into Microsoft 365 E5, later extended to E7. About a dozen new agents embedded in Defender, Entra, Intune and Purview. Microsoft markets the product around agents rather than the earlier chat-assistant framing.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 47 Cybersecurity Operations (best fit: triage, hunting, incident summaries). 48 Security Governance, Risk & Privacy fits via the Purview and Entra agents. 45 IT Infrastructure is a weaker fit, limited to Intune and Entra device and identity tasks.
- **Training notes:** Learners must master prompt scoping, reading agent evidence and verdicts, SCU consumption monitoring, role and permission setup, and the data-sharing setting. Suggested 24-32 hours (Specialist band), including supervised alert-triage labs.
- **Caveats:** Value depends on the Microsoft security stack being deployed. SCU consumption is hard to forecast. The E5 inclusion is phased, so some tenants may not yet have it. The data-sharing default is ON for non-E5/E7 customers and should be reviewed by privacy teams. Outputs can be wrong and need analyst validation.
- **Sources (3):** [Security Copilot pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/pricing/microsoft-security-copilot) · [Security Copilot privacy and data security (Microsoft Learn)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/privacy-data-security) · [Security Copilot inclusion for E5 and E7 (Microsoft Learn)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/security-copilot-inclusion)

**CrowdStrike Charlotte AI** (CrowdStrike (Falcon platform); medium confidence)

- **What it is:** An agentic AI security analyst built into the CrowdStrike Falcon platform. It uses a multi-agent architecture to triage detections, investigate across endpoint, identity and cloud, write queries and generate reports. It is used through the Falcon console, with Charlotte AI AgentWorks for no-code custom agents and Charlotte Agentic SOAR for workflows.
- **Key capabilities:** Detection Triage agent giving a verdict, confidence score and explanation; Natural-language queries, command-line analysis and exposure prioritisation; AgentWorks: no-code custom security agents on a choice of models; Agentic SOAR workflows with configurable human-approval gates; Incident reports and executive summaries via agents, Fusion workflows or promptbooks
- **Enterprise data handling:** Applies to the Falcon platform. CrowdStrike states Charlotte AI is certified to ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AI governance), respects each user's existing permissions, and logs every action. Controls include role-based access, execution traces, agent version history and rollback, credit caps and approval workflows. Prebuilt agents do not take response actions by default. Whether customer data is used for model training, data residency and retention, and SOC 2 or FedRAMP status were not verified on opened pages.
- **Licensing and pricing:** Charlotte AI is metered in monthly AI credits. The vendor page says qualifying Falcon customers get free access with 50 credits that renew monthly. Paid volumes: contact sales, usually through Falcon Flex. The CrowdStrike pricing page was not re-verified for Charlotte AI prices; no Charlotte AI list price was verified.
- **Integrations:** Falcon modules (endpoint, identity, cloud, Next-Gen SIEM, Fusion, Falcon Foundry), bidirectional connections to third-party tools and agents. Relevant to department 47 (alert triage, hunting, incident summaries).
- **2025-2026 developments:** Fal.Con Sept 2025: Forrester reports seven specialist agents (Malware Analysis, Hunt, Exposure Prioritization, Search Analysis, Correlation Rule Generation, Data Transformation, Workflow Generation) and the AgentWorks no-code builder. The vendor page positions Charlotte AI around Agentic SOAR and ISO 42001 certification. Pangea and Onum acquisitions were not re-verified.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 47 Cybersecurity Operations is the only listed department and a strong fit, but only for Falcon customers.
- **Training notes:** Learners must master Falcon data model basics, reading triage verdicts and confidence scores, approval-gate design and credit management. Suggested 24-32 hours (Specialist band).
- **Caveats:** Works best with a Falcon estate; limited value for non-Falcon shops. Credit-based consumption is hard to forecast. The vendor's 98% accuracy figure is a vendor benchmark against its own MDR team. Public pricing for Charlotte AI is not published.
- **Sources (2):** [CrowdStrike Charlotte AI platform page](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/platform/charlotte-ai/) · [Forrester: CrowdStrike Fal.Con 2025](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/crowdstrike-fal-con-2025-flexing-into-the-agentic-ai-age)

**Google Gemini in Security** (Google Cloud (Google Security Operations, Google Threat Intelligenc...; medium confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Google Cloud (Google Security Operations, Google Threat Intelligence, Mandiant; part of Google Unified Security)
- **What it is:** Gemini-powered assistance and agents built into Google Security Operations (SIEM/SOAR) and Google Threat Intelligence. It generates detection rules and search queries, summarises and investigates cases, and runs a Triage and Investigation agent that judges whether alerts are true or false positives.
- **Key capabilities:** Triage and Investigation agent that evaluates alerts and runs an investigation plan; Natural-language search and detection-rule generation; Case summaries and an investigative chat assistant with response recommendations; Threat-intelligence Q&A drawing on Mandiant intelligence; AI agents embedded in SOAR playbooks
- **Enterprise data handling:** Applies to Gemini for Google Cloud. Google's data-governance page states prompts and responses are not used to train Gemini models; optional programmes share data for product improvement only. Gemini in SecOps requests may be routed to any available global region, so strict data residency is not guaranteed. Specific SOC 2, ISO and FedRAMP statements and retention periods for Gemini were not verified.
- **Licensing and pricing:** Google SecOps is sold in Standard, Enterprise and Enterprise Plus packages based on data ingestion, with 12 months of telemetry retention included. Per-package prices: contact sales. Which packages include Gemini features is not verified on an opened page. Google Unified Security bundles Enterprise Plus plus other products (not re-verified).
- **Integrations:** Google SecOps SIEM and SOAR (700+ parsers, 300+ SOAR integrations), Google Threat Intelligence and Mandiant, Wiz for multicloud (Wiz not re-verified). Relevant to department 47.
- **2025-2026 developments:** Cloud Next 2026 (Apr): per CSO Online, threat hunting and detection engineering agents (preview) and a third-party context agent (entering preview). Note the brand is fragmented across Gemini in Security Operations, Gemini in Threat Intelligence and the Agentic SOC.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 47 Cybersecurity Operations (strong fit, mostly for Google SecOps customers).
- **Training notes:** Learners must master YARA-L/UDM search basics, validating agent verdicts, playbook-embedded agents and regional-processing implications. Suggested 24-32 hours (Specialist band).
- **Caveats:** Needs Google SecOps. Gemini features vary by package and preview status. Agent GA/preview status changes quickly.

**Microsoft Purview** (Microsoft (Microsoft Purview data security, governance and complian...; medium confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Microsoft (Microsoft Purview data security, governance and compliance family; includes Copilot in Microsoft Purview)
- **What it is:** A suite for data security, data governance and compliance across Microsoft 365, Azure and other estates. Its AI relevance is twofold: Copilot in Purview and Security Copilot agents assist with alerts and investigations, and Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) for AI governs how Copilots, agents and third-party AI apps handle sensitive data.
- **Key capabilities:** Discover, classify and protect sensitive data, with DLP and insider-risk controls; DSPM for AI covering Microsoft 365 Copilot, Security Copilot, Copilot Studio and third-party generative AI apps; Data governance and compliance management (eDiscovery, retention, communication compliance); Copilot and agents embedded for alert triage and investigation; Audit and compliance of AI interactions
- **Enterprise data handling:** Applies to Purview in Microsoft 365 commercial tenants. Purview inherits Microsoft's compliance programme, but specific attestations (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, FedRAMP) and AI-feature training terms were not verified on opened pages. The Security Copilot privacy documentation covers the Purview-related data Security Copilot reads, such as DLP alerts, and its data-sharing default applies.
- **Licensing and pricing:** On Microsoft's Singapore pricing page (opened and confirmed): Microsoft 365 E5 USD 60.00, E5 without Teams USD 51.45, and the Microsoft Purview Suite add-on USD 12.00 (shown per user; billing term not confirmed). Some Purview capabilities are consumptive (pay-as-you-go; not re-verified). Security Copilot is included in M365 E5 at no additional cost.
- **Integrations:** Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, Defender, Entra, Security Copilot, Fabric and Copilot Studio. Relevant to department 48 (policy mapping, privacy workflows, risk assessments).
- **2025-2026 developments:** The Learn page describes DSPM and DSPM for AI (classic) as the front door for AI-usage governance; the claim that the product page announces a new expanded DSPM was not verified. Purview agents are among the Security Copilot agents bundled into E5 (Nov 2025).
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 48 Security Governance, Risk & Privacy is the only listed department and a strong fit for data classification, retention and AI-usage governance.
- **Training notes:** Learners must master sensitivity labels, DLP policy design, DSPM for AI reports, eDiscovery and retention basics, and licensing scope. Suggested 24-36 hours (Specialist band).
- **Caveats:** Licensing is complex (E5, Purview Suite, pay-as-you-go). AI governance coverage is strongest for Microsoft apps; non-Microsoft AI coverage may be metered. Regional pricing varies (the opened page was the Singapore store).

**Azure Copilot** (Microsoft (Azure; high confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Microsoft (Azure; formerly Microsoft Copilot in Azure)
- **What it is:** An AI companion in the Azure portal and mobile app that answers questions, generates queries and performs actions on your Azure resources. It now includes agents for migration, deployment, optimisation, observability, resiliency and troubleshooting. It acts only within the signed-in user's permissions.
- **Key capabilities:** Natural-language Azure help, query generation and resource actions; Troubleshooting agent for root-cause analysis; Optimization agent for cost and sustainability; Deployment and migration agents (infrastructure planning, app modernisation); Resiliency agent and Observability agent (Azure Monitor)
- **Enterprise data handling:** Applies to Azure tenants. Prompts and responses are not used to train or improve the Azure OpenAI foundation models; they are used to improve Microsoft products only per the FAQ's stated conditions (explicit consent in feedback per the original research). Azure Copilot respects RBAC, Privileged Identity Management and Azure Policy, and asks for confirmation before acting. Agent access is managed at tenant level and is on by default for most tenants, and admins can restrict it to Entra users or groups. Residency, retention periods and compliance attestations were not verified on opened pages.
- **Licensing and pricing:** Capabilities available today are included at no extra cost, except the Observability Agent, which has usage-based charges. Docs state future capabilities may be priced. It is not a per-user licence.
- **Integrations:** Azure Monitor, Azure Migrate, Azure Policy, Entra RBAC, Azure Resource Manager. Relevant to department 43 (infrastructure templates, cloud troubleshooting, cost analysis).
- **2025-2026 developments:** Renamed from 'Microsoft Copilot in Azure' to Azure Copilot (the opened docs use the Azure Copilot name; a dated rename notice was not opened). Current agent status per Learn: Troubleshooting GA, Observability GA, Optimization/Resiliency/Migration preview, Deployment unlabelled. Rollout is gradual and subject to Microsoft's discretion.
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 43 Cloud Infrastructure & Platform Engineering is the only listed department and a good fit for Azure-centric teams. Weak for AWS- or GCP-centric estates.
- **Training notes:** Learners must master reviewing proposed actions before confirming, RBAC scoping, Azure Policy interaction and cost-impact validation. Suggested 20-28 hours (Specialist band).
- **Caveats:** Azure-only. Preview agents may change or be withdrawn; availability is gradual. Capacity throttling is possible. Generated infrastructure changes need change control.
