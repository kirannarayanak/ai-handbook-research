### Sales and marketing AI

The six tools form three layers. Gong is conversation and revenue intelligence that sits on top of the CRM; HubSpot's Breeze Assistant and Agent Hub are AI native to the CRM itself; LinkedIn's Sales Navigator and Hiring Assistant monetise LinkedIn's member graph for prospecting and recruiting; Google Ads AI (AI Max, Performance Max, Ask Advisor) and Meta Advantage+ are opaque optimisation suites inside the two dominant ad platforms. Gong, HubSpot and Sales Navigator overlap on account research and outreach drafting and are increasingly wired together (Gong exposes MCP servers to HubSpot and Microsoft 365 Copilot; Sales Navigator Advanced Plus syncs into HubSpot, Salesforce, Dynamics and Oracle). Google and Meta compete for the same performance budgets and are converging on "give us a URL and a budget" automation while removing manual controls. Enterprises usually standardise on one CRM-native assistant, add Gong for enterprise sales coaching and forecasting, licence LinkedIn seats to sales and talent teams, and treat Google and Meta AI as unavoidable defaults that marketers must learn to steer rather than tools they choose.

*Fact-check:* The Gong, LinkedIn Sales Navigator pricing, HubSpot agent pricing and model-training default, and Google no-subscription and ISO 27001 claims checked out against opened pages. Many fine-grained claims (HubSpot seat pricing, Meta terms and API deprecations, Herohunt GBP rate card, Muse Image availability, Breeze retirement) could not be supported and were removed, softened or marked not verified. Meta's developer and legal pages and HubSpot's pricing pages returned errors or empty text, so Meta is low confidence and the other tools medium. Training-hour estimates and department mappings are editorial, not sourced. 13 correction(s) or removal(s) were applied.

**Gong AI** (Gong.io Inc; medium confidence)

- **Vendor and product family:** Gong.io Inc. Gong markets the platform as the "Gong Revenue AI OS" with modules Gong Engage, Gong Forecast, Gong Enable, Gong Agents and Gong Assistant.
- **What it is:** Gong records and transcribes sales calls, meetings and emails, then uses AI to summarise conversations, flag deal risk, coach reps and forecast revenue. It lives in its own web and mobile app and is surfaced inside Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Teams and Zoom; since February 2026 it also exposes MCP servers so Microsoft 365 Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce and HubSpot agents can query Gong data.
- **Key capabilities:** Call and meeting capture, transcription and AI summaries across Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and telephony; Gong Assistant: conversational Q&A grounded in recorded customer conversations (launched Feb 2026); Gong Forecast: AI deal-risk scoring and pipeline forecasting; Gong Engage: sequences, dialer and AI-drafted follow-ups; Gong Enable: AI Call Reviewer scorecards, AI Trainer role-play and Initiative Tracking; Account Console and Account Boards for renewal-risk and expansion signals; Gong Agents automate CRM edits and follow-ups
- **Enterprise data handling:** Single enterprise contract (no self-serve tier). Gong's trust page states "Your data is never used to train generative AI models" and that customer data is not co-mingled; encryption, MFA and least-privilege access are listed. Default retention is up to three years or the contract duration, whichever is shorter, with custom retention on request and permanent deletion 30 days after termination. The trust page lists SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001:2022, 27017, 27018, 27701, ISO 42001, PCI DSS SAQ-D, CSA STAR and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. HIPAA-related security requirements are listed, but covered-entity status and FedRAMP are not verified. Gong help docs say an admin can choose an EU data centre, whereas a Dec 2025 third-party (competitor Oliv) DPA review claimed US-only storage, so confirm residency contractually. Redaction, deny lists, consent management and SSO details were not re-verified.
- **Licensing and pricing:** No published list prices. The pricing page confirms per-user licences plus a platform fee based on users supported, with quotes from a sales form by team size. Third-party estimate (Docket, May 2026): roughly US$1,400-3,000 per user per year depending on bundle, platform fee US$5,000-50,000, implementation US$15,000-65,000. Contract-term length (2-3 years) is not verified. Not bundled with any suite.
- **Integrations:** Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, Microsoft Dynamics 365; Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet; Slack; Snowflake and BI tools; LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, G2, Gainsight, Chili Piper, Zapier; Microsoft 365 Copilot and other MCP clients; partner ecosystem in the Gong Collective (partner count not verified).
- **2025-2026 developments:** Feb 25 2026 "Mission Andromeda" release: Gong Enable, Gong Assistant, Account Console/Boards and MCP interoperability. Gong's site says it is a Leader again in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Revenue Action Orchestration (earlier Dec 2025 Leader claim not re-verified). VentureBeat reports roughly US$300M ARR and 5,000+ customers. Platform branded "Revenue AI OS" (not a rename of Gong itself).
- **Best-fit handbook departments:** 28 Sales - Enterprise & Key Accounts (Core): strongest fit for call summaries, account research and opportunity planning. 30 Sales Operations & Revenue Operations (Advanced): good for pipeline hygiene and forecast insight, but Docket notes 40% of Gong customers also pay for Clari, so treat forecasting fit as partial.
- **Training notes:** Learners must master call-recording consent and regional recording law, tracker and redaction configuration, critical reading of deal warnings and forecast categories, prompting Gong Assistant and checking answers against transcripts, and CRM hygiene. Core 6-8 hours for sellers; Advanced 12-16 hours for RevOps admins (editorial estimates).
- **Caveats:** Transcription accuracy can degrade with accents; Docket finds Gong Assistant weak on deeper queries; trackers need maintenance; expensive with multi-year terms; pricing opaque. Claims about the EU data centre disabling dialer features, API bulk export and early-termination penalties were not re-verified.
