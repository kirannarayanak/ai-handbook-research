## Additional tools the handbook omits

41 widely adopted enterprise AI tools are absent from the handbook's 57. Candidates were researched by category, fact-checked, and de-duplicated against the handbook list; anything found to be a handbook tool under another name was dropped. Suggested departments use the handbook's numbering.

*General AI assistants and research tools and Microsoft Copilots and Power Platform:* The handbook's general-assistant coverage is vendor-model heavy and omits the enterprise-search and work-AI layer (Glean, Amazon Quick), sovereign/private-deployment vendors (Cohere North, Mistral) and brand-governed writing (Writer). All five candidates exist today and none duplicates a handbook tool, but adoption evidence is thin for Amazon Quick, Mistral and Writer. No Microsoft Copilot or Power Platform candidates survived into this list (Copilot Studio, Power Apps and Dynamics 365 Copilot were mentioned but not proposed or verified). Perplexity and Perplexity Enterprise are listed twice in the existing handbook and could be merged.

*Presentation, design and document tools and Coding and engineering assistants:* Verified against opened pages: Gartner's 20 May 2026 Magic Quadrant for Enterprise AI Coding Agents names Anthropic, Cursor, GitHub and OpenAI as Leaders, AWS, Google, Alibaba Cloud and Cognition as Challengers, Tabnine as the only Visionary, and Atlassian, BytePlus and JetBrains as Niche Players. GitLab was a self-announced Leader in the 2025 predecessor (AI Code Assistants) MQ but is not listed in the 2026 report. AWS and Google moved from Leaders to Challengers, so framing Amazon Q Developer and Gemini Code Assist as leaders is dated. The design and document category lacks agreement management (Docusign), AI workspaces (Notion, Miro), writing agents (Superhuman, formerly Grammarly) and enterprise content production (Adobe GenStudio). The Atlassian Rovo rename of the existing Jira entry was not verified and is out of scope here.

*Enterprise business platforms and Sales and marketing AI:* The handbook covers CRM, HCM, ITSM and ERP assistants (Salesforce, Workday, SAP, ServiceNow) but omits Oracle Fusion's embedded agents, Dynamics 365's agent layer, and the cross-application work-AI layer (Glean, Zoom AI Companion). In sales and marketing it covers conversation intelligence, ads and CRM, but not the revenue orchestration and enablement categories (Gartner MQs for Revenue Action Orchestration and Revenue Enablement Platforms, Dec and Nov 2025; Forrester Wave for Revenue Orchestration Platforms). Naming in the existing list is partly dated (for example Salesforce Einstein now Agentforce; the Jira/Rovo and Agentspace/Gemini Enterprise renames were not verified here). Gemini Enterprise and Agentforce were left out as renames of listed or adjacent tools.

*Customer service and experience AI and Legal, audit and GRC AI:* The handbook has helpdesk and survey AI (Zendesk AI, Intercom Fin, Qualtrics AI, Gainsight AI) but no contact-centre (CCaaS) platform, although the five 2025 Gartner CCaaS Leaders (NiCE, Genesys, AWS, Five9, Talkdesk) are all absent. In legal, audit and GRC it has CoCounsel, AuditBoard AI and Acrobat AI Assistant, but lacks a dedicated legal-AI assistant, CLM, eDiscovery and enterprise GRC/privacy platforms (Harvey, Ironclad, Relativity aiR, Diligent, OneTrust, Icertis). None of the 11 retained candidates duplicates one of the 57 existing tools. Possible naming drift in the existing list (Salesforce Einstein vs Agentforce, Atlassian Intelligence, Azure AI Foundry) was not verified here and should be checked before publishing. Adoption evidence for the Legal/GRC picks is mostly vendor-stated.

*Security, cloud operations and observability AI and Data, analytics and AI development platforms:* The handbook covers security assistants (Security Copilot, Charlotte AI, Gemini in Security, Purview) and two observability tools (Datadog, Dynatrace), but names no SIEM or SecOps platform (Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, Cortex XSIAM) and no open-stack observability platform (Grafana Cloud), though all appear in the 2025 Gartner SIEM or Observability Magic Quadrants. In data and AI it covers Databricks, Snowflake, Bedrock, MLflow and Foundry, but omits Google's agent and ML platform (formerly Vertex AI, rebranded Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform in April 2026 per The Register), Dataiku and Microsoft Fabric. Existing handbook entry 'Azure AI Foundry' is now called Microsoft Foundry (Directions on Microsoft, Nov 2025), so the handbook name is outdated.
