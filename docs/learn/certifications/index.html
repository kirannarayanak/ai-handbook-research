General AI assistants and research tools

Anthropic has the most complete free academy (Claude 101, AI Fluency, Cowork and Claude Code courses) plus a partner-only architect exam. Google offers Skills paths for Gemini Enterprise and Gemini Notebook plus a help center for NotebookLM. OpenAI Academy is open to all, but its certification and Enterprise help pages could not be opened. Perplexity has docs, a help center and a resource hub with short Academy lessons, but no credential.

Fact-check notes · 8 corrections applied

I opened every vendor URL except the two OpenAI pages, which returned 403 (also for a second certifications URL). Claude, Google Skills path 3666, NotebookLM/Gemini Notebook help, Perplexity and OpenAI Academy entries match the pages. Most durations and costs are genuinely not shown on vendor pages, so they remain "not stated". Confidence for ChatGPT Enterprise is low because no certification or Enterprise help page could be confirmed.

All 12 distinct URLs opened successfully. Titles, levels, module or unit counts and exam lengths were checked against the page text. Learn pages do not print a cost, so 'Free' for modules and paths is inferred from Learn's usual model, and exam prices vary by country. The Power BI overview doc opened but showed an authorization notice, so only its title is confirmed.
