Four principles the handbook is built on

Foundation first

Everyone learns one employer-approved general assistant, prompting, verification, privacy and human review before anything role-specific.

Role-based depth

Each role picks two to four tools for practical exercises: a general assistant plus the specialist platform the role already runs.

Three tiers

Core 6-10 hours, Advanced 12-20 hours, Specialist 20-40+ hours. Assessment is on real deliverables, safe data use and accuracy.

Validate before adopting

Licensing, residency, integrations and even product names change monthly. Nothing here is a purchase mandate.
