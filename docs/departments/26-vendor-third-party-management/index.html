AI use cases in practice

  • Reviewing AI and security questionnaires against a use-case-based checklist (what data goes into prompts, whether the vendor trains on it, which model providers and subprocessors are involved) [1].
  • Summarising vendor contracts to find data-use, training, retention and incident-response terms [1]; FINRA suggests contract language barring sensitive data from vendors' open-source GenAI tools [2].
  • Inventorying embedded AI in purchased software and foundation-model dependencies, asking vendors whether they use GenAI [2].
  • Policy retrieval and summarisation across vendor policies, which FINRA observed as common GenAI uses [2].
  • Building supplier performance summaries and scorecard narratives from SLA data (judgment, not source-verified; the Simfoni page was not found to cover SLA scorecards).

Capstone deliverable

A third-party risk file for one mock AI-enabled vendor: AI-assisted questionnaire review, contract-term extraction, risk tier and approval conditions (approved use case, data allowed, monitoring), and a one-page SLA scorecard. Submit prompts, a human-verified findings table and time saved. Sized for Advanced-tier hours.

Data risks and rules

Vendor security reports, SOC reports, pricing and contract terms (confidentiality clauses), personal data in questionnaire responses (GDPR), and in regulated firms third-party risk obligations such as FINRA supervision rules [2]. Rules: a vendor can pass a standard security review and still create AI governance risk, so assess by use case [1]; do not upload vendors' confidential security artefacts to unapproved tools; require human sign-off on risk tiers; track fourth parties and subprocessors [1].

Adoption KPIs

Time to complete a vendor risk assessment; percentage of vendors with AI usage declared and risk-tiered; share of contracts with data-use and training clauses checked; open findings aging; SLA breaches detected per quarter.

Curriculum emphasis

Hours go to AI vendor due diligence and contract extraction, with scorecard reporting second. Prioritise Claude (long contract and report reading), ServiceNow Now Assist (where the vendor register sits; not verified in opened sources), and Power BI Copilot for scorecards. Teach evaluating vendors' own AI by use case [1].

Tool-list fit

Moderate fit. ServiceNow Now Assist is right if the vendor workflow runs on ServiceNow (not verified in opened sources). Gemini and Microsoft 365 Copilot are generic. Omitted: dedicated third-party risk and GRC platforms, such as SmartSuite-style AI vendor risk workflows [1].
