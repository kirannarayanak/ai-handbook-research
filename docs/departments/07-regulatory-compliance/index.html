Department 07 · Leadership, strategy and governance

Regulatory Compliance

Regulatory change summaries, control evidence, compliance checklists

Specialist · 20-40+ hMedium confidenceLeadership, strategy and governance
Compare this department Show in matrix

Recommended tools

From the handbook. Tier Specialist, 20-40+ h per tool as a planning band.

ClaudeGeminiNotebookLMMicrosoft 365 CopilotServiceNow Now AssistThomson Reuters CoCounsel

AI use cases in practice

  • Automated regulatory change management: scanning global regulatory sources, identifying relevant changes and mapping obligations to internal policies, risks and controls [1]
  • Control harmonisation: finding duplicate or overlapping controls across frameworks to reduce testing burden [1]
  • Dynamic policy mapping against new requirements, highlighting gaps quickly [1]
  • Co-pilots for compliance research and drafting regulator-ready reports; AI-enabled complaints management (not individually verified) [1]
  • General counsels and compliance officers using GenAI for compliance workloads: in a CSC survey of 350, 97% had adopted it and 72% had governance policies in place [2]

Capstone deliverable

A regulatory-change brief for one real recent rule: summary, obligation-to-control mapping table, gap list and a compliance checklist, with citations to the primary regulator text, prompts, a reviewer sign-off, and time saved against a manual baseline. Specialist-tier hours.

Data risks and rules

Regulator correspondence, examination findings, control evidence, and personal data in monitoring (GDPR); some work touches SOX, PCI DSS, HIPAA or sector rules depending on the firm. Rules: human-in-the-loop review is described by a vendor commentator as a regulatory expectation and responsibility cannot be delegated entirely to AI [1]; be able to show how outputs were produced and validated; cite primary regulatory text, not AI summaries. The claim that regulators are also using AI is not verified.

Adoption KPIs

Days from regulation publication to impact assessment; share of obligations mapped to controls; compliance-checklist errors found in review; hours of manual monitoring replaced.

Curriculum emphasis

Prioritise Thomson Reuters CoCounsel (research; not verified by an opened source), Claude and NotebookLM (document-grounded regulatory comparison), and ServiceNow Now Assist if controls evidence sits in ServiceNow. Spend time on audit-ready documentation of AI use.

Tool-list fit

Fits reasonably. Smaller specialised models were described as more reliable than public models for compliance research [1], so Gemini as a general tool is a weaker fit. The handbook omits dedicated regtech platforms such as 4CRisk.ai, whose COO is the source of the commentary [1] (vendor-sourced).
