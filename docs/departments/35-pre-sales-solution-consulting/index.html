Department 35 · Sales, marketing and business development

Pre-Sales & Solution Consulting

RFP responses, solution proposals, demo preparation

AI use cases in practice

  • Security questionnaire automation: reviewers edit AI drafts from an approved content library; Fiddler AI cut a 30-hour questionnaire to 3-4 hours (87%), vendor-reported [1].
  • RFP first drafts at scale: SugarAI reports 90% of initial response work automated and roughly 12-18 questions per RFP still routed to SMEs, vendor-reported [2].
  • Freeing solution consultants from searching and drafting so they focus on solution strategy and customer engagement (SugarAI's Director, Solution Consulting) [2].
  • Personalised demos: AI tailors demo messaging by persona and topic and cuts demo creation from days to minutes (vendor claim) [3].
  • Prospect-specific demo data: a tool generates demo records from a discovery call and injects them into the live app (vendor page) [4].
  • Conversation intelligence on discovery and demo calls to surface objections and improve demo prep [3].

Capstone deliverable

A response-and-demo kit for one realistic RFP excerpt (at least 25 questions): AI-drafted answers each tagged with an approved source or flagged for SME review, a tailored solution proposal outline, and a demo script with persona-specific talking points. Include a prompt log and measured time saved against a manual baseline. About 8 hours at Advanced tier; assessed on factual accuracy and correct flagging of unsupported claims.

Data risks and rules

Customer confidential data, pricing, unreleased roadmap, security architecture details and NDA material. RFP answers can become contractual commitments, so unverified AI claims about security, compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR) or features create legal exposure. Rules: draft only from approved content, route technical and legal claims to SMEs, never paste customer data into unapproved tools, and keep a human sign-off. Frequent hallucinations (plus missing workflow features) led Fiddler to replace its homegrown chatbot [1].

Adoption KPIs

Hours per RFP or questionnaire vs baseline; share of answers needing minimal edits; RFPs answered per headcount; SME minutes per response; demo prep time [1][2].

Curriculum emphasis

Advanced tier: put most hours on grounded drafting with citations, review workflows and demo preparation. Prioritise Claude (long RFP documents and structured drafts), Microsoft 365 Copilot (internal content and meeting notes), Salesforce Einstein (opportunity context) and Gamma (proposal and demo decks). Teach SME routing and claim verification.

Tool-list fit

The list is general-purpose and omits dedicated RFP and questionnaire platforms (AutoRFP.ai is evidenced in [1][2]; Loopio and Responsive were not opened, so not verified) and demo platforms (Demoboost [3], Reprise [4]). Gong is listed by a vendor article as a presales tool [3] but is not on this department's list. Salesforce Einstein fits opportunity context but no presales-specific evidence was found for it. Gamma is a weak fit for technical answer accuracy.
