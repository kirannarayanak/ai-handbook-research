Department 21 · Finance, procurement and investor relations

Accounting & Financial Reporting

Reconciliation assistance, journal explanations, reporting drafts

Advanced · 12-20 hHigh confidenceFinance, procurement and investor relations
Compare this department Show in matrix

Recommended tools

From the handbook. Tier Advanced, 12-20 h per tool as a planning band.

ClaudeGeminiExcel CopilotMicrosoft 365 CopilotSAP Joule

AI use cases in practice

  • Describing a transaction in plain English and having GenAI draft a multi-line journal entry against the chart of accounts, turning data entry into review and approval [1].
  • Uploading bank and ledger datasets so the model identifies matches, discrepancies and unrecorded items, then drafting control-account reconciliation summaries [1].
  • Estimating accruals for services received but not yet invoiced from an anonymised service contract, with accountant review [1]; SAP has released an Accounting Accruals Agent in Joule [3].
  • Variance analysis on a trial balance with first-draft commentary and draft footnote disclosures from supplied data and the relevant standard [1].
  • Close agents that prepare reconciliations and draft journals for human sign-off, from vendors such as BlackLine and FloQast [2].

Capstone deliverable

A month-end mini-close on synthetic data: five reconciliations and three journal entries drafted with AI, plus draft variance commentary. Deliver a control memo covering agent scope and named owner, retained inputs and reasoning, reviewer sign-off per item, error rate found on review, and measured time saved against manual preparation. Sized for Advanced-tier hours.

Data risks and rules

Unpublished financial statements (material non-public information), data feeding financial reporting under SOX internal control over financial reporting, and supplier, customer and employee personal data inside ledgers (GDPR). Rules: every AI output feeding the statements needs a human approval step scaled to materiality; keep an inventory of AI tools with a named owner; retain inputs, steps, exceptions and match basis so a reviewer or auditor can replay them; check SOC reports for what they say about AI components [2]. COSO issued guidance on internal control over generative AI in February 2026 [2]. Anonymise contracts before upload [1].

Adoption KPIs

Days to close; percentage of reconciliations AI-prepared and passed at first review; reviewer-found error rate on AI drafts (set an exit threshold, as recommended) [2]; manual journal entry preparation hours; share of AI tools with a documented owner and evidence standard [2].

Curriculum emphasis

Weight hours to controls and review, then to close tasks. Prioritise Excel Copilot (reconciliations, variance on trial balances), SAP Joule (where SAP is the ledger, for accruals and embedded agents), and Claude or Microsoft 365 Copilot for contract reading and journal explanations. Vendor match-rate claims are marketing until your own data reproduces them [2].

Tool-list fit

Fit is good for ERP-centred teams: Excel Copilot and SAP Joule match the work, and Joule applies only if the company runs SAP [3]. Gemini adds little beyond Claude and Microsoft 365 Copilot in a Microsoft shop (judgment). Omitted: dedicated close and reconciliation platforms such as BlackLine and FloQast, plus Microsoft's Dynamics 365 reconciliation agent in preview [2].
