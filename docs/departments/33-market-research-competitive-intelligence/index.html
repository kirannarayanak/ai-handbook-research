AI use cases in practice

  • Cited competitive research and battlecards: Ramp product marketing turns source-backed answers into battlecards for sales [1]
  • Early-stage market briefings validated by analysts: Latham's research team reports a better product in the same time, not mainly time saved [2]
  • Synthetic respondents for testing messaging and packaging: 71% of 3,000+ researchers surveyed by Qualtrics expect most research to use synthetic responses within 3 years; 87% of users report high satisfaction (vendor survey) [3]
  • AI adoption in competitive intelligence is accelerating but held back by data quality, privacy and expertise concerns (109-respondent life sciences survey) [4]
  • Recurring monitoring prompts reused for single-company research [2]

Capstone deliverable

A competitor brief on one named market: 5+ claims each traced to an opened, dated source, one NotebookLM or Perplexity-grounded synthesis, a validation log marking unverified items, documented prompts, and a time comparison. Core-tier size.

Data risks and rules

Survey and panel data can contain personal data (GDPR); competitor intelligence must be gathered lawfully (no misrepresentation or breach of confidentiality, trade-secret and competition-law limits; not verified on an opened page); client research may be under NDA. Synthetic data must be labelled and not used as proof of real behaviour. Rules: public sources only unless cleared, cite every claim, no confidential client data in public tools.

Adoption KPIs

Share of claims with verified sources; turnaround time for a briefing; stakeholder satisfaction score; number of recurring monitors running; hallucination or correction rate found in review.

Curriculum emphasis

Most hours on source verification and triangulation. Prioritise Perplexity Enterprise (cited answers), NotebookLM (source-grounded synthesis), Claude for long-document analysis, and Power BI Copilot only for survey dashboards.

Tool-list fit

Strong fit for Perplexity (source-backed answers) [1][2]; NotebookLM not verified on an opened page. Power BI Copilot is a weaker fit for qualitative research. Omission: dedicated survey and market-intelligence platforms such as Qualtrics (research-AI vendor) are not listed [3]; competitor-monitoring tools not verified.
