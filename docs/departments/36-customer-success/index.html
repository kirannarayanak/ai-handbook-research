Customer Success

Customer health, renewal risks, success plans

AI use cases in practice

  • Continuous account health monitoring: an AI agent in the CRM combines product adoption, usage velocity, support case frequency, executive engagement and contract milestones, flags anomalies and proposes actions to the CSM (tool type: CRM-embedded agent; outcome: earlier renewal-risk detection, consultancy-described, not measured) [1]
  • Next-best-action drafting: the agent recommends outreach, content to share or internal escalation, for example a technical enablement session for an account struggling with advanced features [1]
  • Routine task automation: renewal reminders, health-check emails and meeting preparation partially automated by AI so CSMs spend time on high-impact accounts [1]
  • Meeting-derived sentiment: a Gainsight customer-story page reportedly adds meeting-summary data to sentiment scoring (page could not be opened, not verified)
  • Support-signal enrichment: support-case frequency and resolution time are fed into the account profile so CSMs see service friction before a renewal conversation [1]
  • CX benchmark context for CS conversations: Zendesk reports 22% higher customer retention among 'CX Trendsetters' (vendor survey, treat as indicative) [2]

Capstone deliverable

A renewal-risk brief pack for 5 anonymised or synthetic accounts. For each account: a Claude or Gemini draft health summary, a human-corrected risk rating with evidence, a success plan, and a renewal email draft. Submit with the prompt log, a source/validation table (every claim traced to a CRM field or call note) and measured time saved against a manual baseline. Sized for about 12-16 Advanced-tier hours.

Data risks and rules

Customer contact details, contract values and pricing, usage telemetry, support transcripts and stakeholder names. GDPR/UK GDPR applies to named contacts and call notes (lawful basis, minimisation, DPIA for profiling). Contracts often carry confidentiality clauses. Rules: use only enterprise-approved tools; strip personal data when pasting outside the CRM; never let AI send customer-facing messages or alter commercial terms without human review; keep customer data in the CRM's governed agent where possible. Zendesk reports shadow AI use (external tools unapproved for agent use) jumping up to 250% year on year in some industries and links this to privacy, security and service-quality risk [2].

Adoption KPIs

Share of at-risk accounts flagged at least 60 days before renewal; CSM prep time per QBR/renewal (minutes); percentage of AI-drafted outreach edited and approved versus rejected; gross revenue retention on accounts using AI health review versus control; weekly active AI users among CSMs.

Curriculum emphasis

Spend most hours on account research and synthesis, health-signal interpretation, and drafting with human review, with a short module on data handling. Prioritise Salesforce Einstein (CRM-native signals, per the Agentforce description [1]), Claude (long call and email summarisation), Microsoft 365 Copilot (meeting and email work) and Gainsight AI (the dedicated CS platform, if licensed). Gamma and Gemini can be covered briefly.

Tool-list fit

The list fits well: Salesforce Einstein and Gainsight AI are the CS-native tools. Gamma is a weak fit, useful only for QBR decks. The list omits Salesforce Agentforce by name; the RSM article describes Agentforce as the renewal/health agent in Salesforce [1], so confirm whether 'Einstein' in the handbook is meant to cover it. Gainsight's Staircase AI is not on the list (not verified from an opened page).
