AI use cases in practice

How teams like this actually use AI today, each with its source.

  • Turning plain-language descriptions into working automations in UiPath Studio via Autopilot, with the vendor claiming 50% faster delivery (vendor claim). [1]
  • Capturing workflows from live systems and translating business requirements into BPMN designs (Autopilot in Maestro) for process mapping. [1]
  • Generating tests from requirements and analysing results to keep automation quality high. [1]
  • Monitoring queues and schedules, surfacing failures early and resolving incidents in plain language. [1]
  • SOP drafting from process maps or recordings with an LLM: not verified.

Capstone deliverable

The one real piece of work a learner produces to pass. It follows the handbook's assignment template.

One end-to-end process improvement package. It contains an AS-IS process map, an AI-assisted TO-BE design, a draft SOP reviewed against actual practice by a process owner, and an automation or workflow specification. The package includes a documented prompt log and a measured cycle-time or effort estimate. Advanced-tier hours.

Data risks and rules

The sensitive data this team handles, the law that applies, and the rules that follow.

Process documentation exposes internal controls (relevant to SOX where financial processes are mapped), customer or employee data in sample records, and credentials in recordings. Rules: use synthetic or masked data in prompts, route automations through governed platforms, and require owner sign-off on SOPs. UiPath states Autopilot actions can be recorded through the AI Trust Layer in a GDPR-compliant manner and that Autopilot uses only admin-approved tools [1]; this is a vendor statement, not a compliance guarantee.

Adoption KPIs

Numbers to track so you know whether the training changed anything.

Number of processes mapped or documented per quarter; SOP drafting time; automations delivered per builder; rework rate of AI-drafted SOPs; percentage of automations built with governed AI assistance.

Curriculum emphasis

Where the hours should go, and which tools to prioritise.

Prioritise UiPath Autopilot and Power Automate (build and governance), Claude (process analysis, SOP writing), and Microsoft 365 Copilot (document and meeting capture). Emphasise validation of AI-drawn maps against observed work.

Tool-list fit

Whether the handbook's tool list suits this team, and what it misses.

Good fit; UiPath Autopilot is confirmed as an assistant for building, operating and troubleshooting automations [1]. Gamma is a weak fit (presentation, not process work). Omissions not verified: process-mining and mapping tools (for example Celonis, Signavio); the UiPath page itself lists Process Intelligence and Maestro as related products [1].

Jargon on this page

  • PromptThe instruction or question you give an AI tool. Clear prompts with context and a desired format get better results.
  • VerifiedVerified means a researcher opened the page and read the claim on it. Not verified means it came from a search snippet or a page that would not load, so treat it with care.
  • Vendor claimA figure published by the company that sells the tool. It may be true, but no one independent has checked it.
  • CopilotMicrosoft's name for its AI assistants inside Word, Excel, Teams, Power BI, security tools and Azure. One licence covers several of them.
