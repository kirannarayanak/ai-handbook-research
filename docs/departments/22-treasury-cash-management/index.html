Department 22 · Finance, procurement and investor relations

Treasury & Cash Management

Cash flow scenarios, liquidity reporting, treasury analysis

Specialist · 20-40+ hMedium confidenceFinance, procurement and investor relations
Compare this department Show in matrix

Recommended tools

From the handbook. Tier Specialist, 20-40+ h per tool as a planning band.

ClaudeGeminiExcel CopilotPower BI CopilotChatGPT Enterprise

AI use cases in practice

  • Cash flow forecasting from daily bank balances plus open receivables and payables, producing a baseline and range and showing which regions miss most, so the treasurer reviews misses rather than collecting files [2].
  • FX exposure mapping: reading open invoices and purchase orders into currencies and settlement dates and refreshing a net exposure report daily; hedge decisions stay human [2].
  • Payment fraud screening that flags changed bank details and scores payments against vendor history, with independent release and call-back controls [2].
  • Drafting a daily cash position note from bank statements, with reviewer tie-out to bank balances [2].
  • Generative AI for cash positioning: Deloitte's 2024 survey of 213 organisations found over 70% saw cash forecasting, and 50% cash positioning, as the most impactful use cases (as summarised by HighRadius) [1].

Capstone deliverable

A 13-week cash forecast pack on synthetic multi-currency data: AI-assisted baseline and range, a back-test of forecast error against actuals by horizon, a net FX exposure table, and a one-page position note. Include documented prompts, a rule list of what the tool may never do (move money, commit hedges), and time saved. Sized for Specialist-tier hours.

Data risks and rules

Bank account numbers and credentials, payment instructions, covenant and liquidity positions (often material non-public information), counterparty data, and card data if in scope (PCI DSS). Business email compromise affected 74% of US organisations in AFP's 2026 payments fraud survey [2]. Rules: no bank details or credentials in prompts; AI never releases payments or changes bank mandates; dual authorisation and call-back for account changes [2]; treat AI-generated hedge or funding suggestions as drafts under the treasury policy. AFP's 2026 benchmarking survey scored AI and emerging-technology policies lowest of any treasury policy area [2], so write one first.

Adoption KPIs

Forecast error by horizon versus pre-AI baseline (only 18% rated their forecasting capability above average or best in class in Deloitte's survey) [1]; hours spent assembling the weekly forecast; share of forecast files received via automated feed rather than spreadsheet; fraud flags raised and confirmed; policy exceptions logged.

Curriculum emphasis

Specialist hours should go to forecasting accuracy measurement and payment-control design. Prioritise Excel Copilot for scenario and position work, Power BI Copilot for liquidity reporting, and Claude for reading bank, loan and covenant documents. Include a controls module on never letting AI release funds [2].

Tool-list fit

Partial fit. Excel Copilot and Power BI Copilot suit reporting, but Microsoft advises avoiding Excel Copilot for decisions in sensitive areas such as finance [3]. Gemini and ChatGPT Enterprise are generic and weaker for forecasting, which sources describe with ML or predictive tools [2]. Omitted: treasury management systems and dedicated cash-forecasting tools, such as HighRadius cash forecasting and the TMS layer, which Deloitte links to cash positioning [1].
