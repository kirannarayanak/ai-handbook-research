AI use cases in practice

How teams like this actually use AI today, each with its source.

  • Google AI Max for Search: Google claims about 14% more conversions at similar CPA/ROAS, with independent analyses more mixed [1]
  • Meta Advantage+ and Performance Max automate targeting; platform ROAS of about 4.5x for Advantage+ Shopping versus a Haus study of 640 incrementality tests showing underperformance versus manual campaigns over time (as reported by Pixis, a vendor) [2]
  • Responsive search ad copy and headline variants exported to upload-ready files, cutting a 30-minute task to about 30 seconds [3]
  • Keyword research chained across SEO tools and pushed into Google Ads (Ramp brand marketing using Perplexity's Comet AI browser) [4]
  • Independent measurement of platform-reported vs actual customer acquisition cost [2]

Capstone deliverable

The one real piece of work a learner produces to pass. It follows the handbook's assignment template.

A test plan and performance report for one automated campaign: hypothesis, holdout or incrementality design, AI-generated ad variants, platform-reported vs independently measured CPA, documented prompts and a recommendation. Advanced-tier depth.

Data risks and rules

The sensitive data this team handles, the law that applies, and the rules that follow.

Audience and conversion data is personal data (GDPR/ePrivacy consent, cookies and tracking under PECR; the cited ICO page was verified only for PECR direct-marketing rules, cookies not verified [5]); customer lists uploaded to ad platforms need a lawful basis. Spend decisions carry financial risk and AI-generated claims need substantiation. Rules: no raw customer lists in general AI tools, guardrails on automated budget changes, human sign-off on claims and spend.

Adoption KPIs

Numbers to track so you know whether the training changed anything.

Incremental ROAS or CPA vs holdout; time to launch a creative test; share of campaigns with an incrementality check; creative refresh cycle time; brand-safety incident count.

Curriculum emphasis

Where the hours should go, and which tools to prioritise.

Emphasise measurement discipline over platform defaults. Prioritise Google Ads AI and Meta Advantage+ (with their opacity limits), HubSpot AI for attribution and reporting, and Gemini for analysis. Canva and Gamma are for creative variants only.

Tool-list fit

Whether the handbook's tool list suits this team, and what it misses.

Google Ads AI and Advantage+ fit, but both are opaque and platform-reported numbers can differ from true acquisition cost [2]; Pixis is one vendor selling independent measurement and is not listed (it is itself a vendor) [2]. Per Xpert.Digital, Google will discontinue Dynamic Search Ads as a standalone format and migrate campaigns to AI Max (postponed to February 2027), so AI Max should be added [1]. Gamma is a weak fit. Claude is not listed though used for ad copy [3].

Jargon on this page

  • PromptThe instruction or question you give an AI tool. Clear prompts with context and a desired format get better results.
  • VerifiedVerified means a researcher opened the page and read the claim on it. Not verified means it came from a search snippet or a page that would not load, so treat it with care.
  • GDPRThe EU's data protection law. Any AI use involving information about identifiable people has to respect it.
