Service Delivery & Managed Services

SLA reporting, service desk optimization, incident patterns

Advanced · 12-20 hMedium confidenceOperations, PMO and transformation

From the handbook. Tier Advanced, 12-20 h per tool as a planning band.

GeminiClaudeServiceNow Now AssistMicrosoft 365 CopilotPower BI Copilot

AI use cases in practice

  • A virtual service agent handles tier-one requests such as password resets and software access, deflecting tickets; Thumbtack reports 15% of requests handled without human involvement and SLA compliance raised from 90% to 95% on Jira Service Management (customer case study, vendor-published) [1].
  • The virtual agent gathers initial details so tickets arrive pre-populated, and offers 24/7 availability (home-services IT director interviewed in a Forrester TEI study) [2].
  • Agent-assist features: issue summarisation, response generation, sentiment analysis and recommended actions to cut handling effort [2].
  • AIOps-style support for incident and change management to make better-informed decisions [2].
  • Intent and template suggestions generated from historical request data to speed service-desk setup and expose service gaps [2].
  • Not verified: ServiceNow Now Assist case summaries and resolution notes (documentation pages did not load).

Capstone deliverable

A monthly SLA and incident-pattern report for one real (anonymised) queue: AI-drafted narrative, a table of the top 5 recurring incident themes, and 3 proposed knowledge articles. Includes a validation log checking every figure against the source export, the documented prompts, and measured time saved versus the manual report (Advanced tier hours).

Data risks and rules

Tickets contain employee and customer personal data (GDPR), credentials or system details pasted by users, and contractual SLA and pricing terms. Rules: use only the approved in-platform or enterprise AI, strip personal data and secrets before pasting elsewhere, never share client-confidential data across client tenants, and have a human verify SLA figures before they go into customer reports. Forrester interviewees noted AI quality depends on knowledge content, so keep articles current [2].

Adoption KPIs

Virtual-agent deflection or auto-resolution rate; SLA compliance percentage; agent handling time per ticket; share of tickets with AI-assisted summary accepted unedited; knowledge articles created or refreshed per month.

Curriculum emphasis

Spend most hours on hands-on work with the service platform's embedded AI (ServiceNow Now Assist where ServiceNow is the platform) for summarisation and knowledge, then Power BI Copilot for SLA reporting, and Claude or Gemini for incident-pattern analysis on exported, anonymised data. Prioritise verification habits, since automation outcomes depend on data and knowledge quality.

Tool-list fit

ServiceNow Now Assist fits where ServiceNow is the ITSM platform, but the opened evidence is for Jira Service Management, which the handbook omits for this department [1][2]. Jira Service Management's AI is used for service desks (Thumbtack, Forrester TEI study; both vendor-commissioned or vendor-published). Ensure the training covers whichever ITSM platform the learner actually uses. Microsoft 365 Copilot and Gemini are general-purpose and not evidenced by these sources.
