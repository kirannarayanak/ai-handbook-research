AI use cases in practice

  • Partner discovery and matching: AI analyses firmographic, technographic, performance and regional data to recommend which partners to recruit or co-market with. Described by an agency as an expected capability, not a measured result [1].
  • Co-sell routing: AI predicts which partners can influence a deal and suggests introductions, replacing manual deal registration [1]. Futurum also reports AWS and Dell using algorithms to select co-sell partners [2].
  • Partner operations agents for onboarding, partner portals, demo environments and deal registration, reported by Futurum from vendor briefings at AWS, Dell, Google Cloud, IBM, Lenovo, Salesforce and ServiceNow [2].
  • Generative AI drafts partner-specific one-pagers and outreach; an unnamed software company in a consultancy snapshot saw more opportunities with an engaged partner and better co-sell win rates (anecdotal, unquantified) [1].
  • Systematic partner prospecting and outreach, with a data-enrichment tool (Clay) feeding an AI qualification and messaging tool (Octave) (vendor how-to, unverified results) [4].
  • Partner targeting is shifting: IDC reports partner value moving toward AI solution design, governance and compliance services and industry advisory [3]. That this should change which partners BD targets is an inference, not stated by IDC.

Capstone deliverable

A partner shortlist and proposal pack for one real or simulated target segment. It contains 5 ranked candidate partners with cited fit rationale, a relationship map, a one-page joint proposal, and a prompt log with time saved against a manual baseline. A reviewer checks that every firmographic or market claim traces to a validated source. About 4 hours at Core tier.

Data risks and rules

Partner and prospect commercial terms, pricing, pipeline and NDA-covered material, plus contact personal data (GDPR/CCPA for outreach and relationship mapping). Do not paste NDA or deal-registration data into unapproved tools. Check lawful basis before profiling individuals. Verify AI-sourced partner facts before they appear in proposals. Any shared multi-party data needs agreed data-sharing policies [1].

Adoption KPIs

Time to identify and shortlist partners vs baseline; partner activation rate; share of opportunities with an engaged partner; co-sell win rate and deal velocity; proposal turnaround time [1].

Curriculum emphasis

Core tier: spend most hours on prompt-driven research and proposal drafting with verification, and on data-handling rules. Prioritise Claude (long-document proposals and synthesis), Microsoft 365 Copilot (internal files and meetings), LinkedIn Sales Navigator AI (relationship mapping) and ChatGPT Enterprise only if it is the approved tool. Cover source checking, since the evidence stresses human judgement remains essential [1].

Tool-list fit

Sales Navigator AI fits relationship mapping (not evidenced in the cited sources). Gamma is a weak fit (presentation polish rather than partner analysis) and is mostly a convenience. The list omits partner-relationship tooling (CRM/PRM and partner-data platforms, named in [1]) and a data-enrichment tool (Clay is cited in [4]); Crossbeam and PartnerStack were not opened, so their fit is not verified.
