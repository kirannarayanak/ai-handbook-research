Beyond the handbook

41 more functions with documented AI use

Functions large organisations commonly staff that the handbook's 60 departments do not cover, found under seven research lenses, fact-checked and de-duplicated. Each carries a starting tool list, the evidence, and the data it handles.

Corporate Communications & Public Relations

All large companies, especially listed, consumer-facing and regulated firms.

CoreMedium confidencePass 1
Why it is missing
No listed department owns media relations, reputation, executive messaging and crisis communications; Marketing & Brand and the Office of the CEO touch it only partly.
Workplace application
Drafting press releases and executive statements, media and sentiment monitoring, crisis Q&A preparation.
Evidence of AI use
Opened: a Wadds Inc. management briefing lists AI use cases in corporate communications and PR (content creation, brainstorming, research, media pitching) and flags risks of unauthorised AI use. Opened: USC Annenberg's 2025 Relevance Report (published 30 Dec 2024), curated with Microsoft, gathers views of over 40 communications leaders on using AI in daily team work. Both pages are descriptive summaries, not adoption statistics.
Data risks
Unreleased announcements, market-sensitive information, embargoed material and crisis plans.
