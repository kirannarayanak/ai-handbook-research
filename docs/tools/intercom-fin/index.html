What it is

An autonomous AI customer agent that resolves support conversations over chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, voice and Slack. It runs natively in the Intercom help desk or on other help desks including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk and Freshdesk. Fin reports a 76% average resolution rate (vendor claim).

Vendor and product family
Fin (the company formerly Intercom, renamed 12 May 2026), acquired by Salesforce on 10 Sept 2026 and operating within Salesforce AI Labs. The help desk keeps the Intercom name (relaunched as Intercom 2).
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

  • Autonomous resolutions grounded in knowledge content
  • Procedures: multi-step workflows that act in external systems
  • Operator back-office agent (in the Pro add-on) and voice
  • Testing and simulation before launch
  • Copilot for human agents; Fin for Sales lead qualification

Enterprise data handling

Verified on Intercom security page: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 42001, HIPAA, AIUC-1 for Fin, GDPR/CCPA; hosting in US, EU or Australia; SSO (Okta, Azure AD, OneLogin), SCIM, 2FA, IP restrictions. 'Fin can use anonymized customer data for model fine-tuning, and you can opt out at any time, with data deleted within 30 days'; third-party AI providers are contractually restricted from training, with zero retention.

Licensing and pricing

Verified: $0.99 per outcome; on an existing help desk, fin.ai/pricing shows a $49/month minimum that includes 50 outcomes (earlier 'no platform fee' wording corrected); 14-day free trial. Intercom help desk seats, annual: Essential $19 ($29 monthly), Advanced $85, Expert $132; Copilot $29/agent/mo annual ($35 monthly); Pro add-on from $100/mo. $9.99 per sales qualification is stated only on a third-party guide (Macha); Fin Voice custom pricing and 'Salesforce packaging not announced' not verified.

Integrations

Intercom help desk natively; Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot and Freshdesk as standalone agent; channels incl. WhatsApp, SMS, email, voice, Slack.

2025-2026 developments

12 May 2026: Intercom renamed company to Fin (verified). 10 Sept 2026: Salesforce completed acquisition; Fin continues within Salesforce AI Labs (verified on Salesforce release). Fin 4 and Apex 2.0 are mentioned on vendor pages.

Training notes

Knowledge-source curation, writing Procedures with guardrails, pre-launch testing, handoff rules, reading outcome reports, Copilot use, deciding on the fine-tuning opt-out. Core 6-10 hours for agents, 12-16 for Fin administrators.

Caveats

Billable 'assumed resolutions' make costs track volume; ownership change may alter roadmap and packaging; anonymized fine-tuning is on unless opted out; Fin for Sales only available on Intercom deployments per third-party guide; the official page's $49/month minimum matches third-party guides.
