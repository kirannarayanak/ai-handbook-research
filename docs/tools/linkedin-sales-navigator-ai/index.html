What it is

A prospecting platform over LinkedIn's member graph that adds generative summaries of accounts and leads, AI-drafted messages and an agent (beta) that recommends leads. On Advanced Plus it is embedded in CRMs (per ZoomInfo).

Vendor and product family
LinkedIn Corporation (Microsoft), LinkedIn Sales Solutions. AI features are marketed as Account IQ, Lead IQ, Message Assist, Buyer Intent and Sales Assistant (beta).
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

  • Account IQ company briefs and Lead IQ person briefs
  • Message Assist AI InMail drafts (public beta; English-only and 50/month cap not re-verified)
  • Sales Assistant (beta)
  • Buyer Intent scoring from 180+ signals (ZoomInfo)
  • AI-assisted and conversational search

Enterprise data handling

LinkedIn's trust page lists ISO 27001 and ISO 27018 covering Sales Solutions; SOC 2 Type II coverage is stated to be in the Smart Trust Center and was not verified for Sales Navigator specifically. Statements about Azure OpenAI use and no training on user data, and Admin Center features, were not re-verified. No data-residency, HIPAA or FedRAMP statements found.

Licensing and pricing

Per LinkedIn's compare-plans page: Core starts at US$119.99 per month or US$1,079.88 per year; Advanced starts at US$159.99 per month or US$1,799.88 per year (also AUD, CAD and other currencies). Advanced Plus is custom-priced via sales; ZoomInfo cites 10+ seats and Advanced 2+ seats. Compare page lists Account IQ and Lead IQ and Message Assist as feature rows (plan allocation per row not re-verified). Not bundled with Microsoft licences: not verified.

Integrations

Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, HubSpot, Oracle Sales on Advanced Plus (per ZoomInfo; Oracle date not re-verified). Claims of no public API and a closed partner programme are not verified.

2025-2026 developments

Sales Assistant is listed as beta in ZoomInfo's Aug 2026 review. Other dated rollout claims (May 2025 launch, Oct 2025 Oracle integration, June 2026 coverage) were not re-verified. Still marketed as Sales Navigator.

Best-fit handbook departments

34 Business Development & Partnerships (Core): strong fit for partner discovery, relationship mapping and first-touch outreach.

Listed for these departments in the handbook

34 Business Development & Partnerships

Training notes

Master ICP and saved-search design, verifying AI briefs, InMail etiquette, CRM sync field mapping and GDPR/ePrivacy rules for outreach. Core 6-8 hours (editorial estimate).

Caveats

Several AI features are beta; intent signals limited to LinkedIn's ecosystem; summaries can be stale; Core has no team administration (not re-verified).
