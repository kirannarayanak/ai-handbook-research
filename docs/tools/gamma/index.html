Gamma Tech, Inc

Gamma

Learning path and certifications

No official course or certification was found for this tool at the time of research. Start with the vendor documentation linked in the sources, then the shared foundation module.

What it is

An AI-native browser app (plus iOS/Android) that generates presentations ('gammas'), documents, websites, social posts and, since March 2026, standalone graphics from a prompt or imported files. Also reachable from ChatGPT, Claude and other tools via connectors/API (TechCrunch, Mar 2026).

Vendor and product family
Gamma Tech, Inc. (San Francisco). Products: Gamma web/mobile app, Gamma Imagine graphics, Gamma API and MCP connectors.
Handbook category
Presentation, design & documents

Key capabilities

  • Prompt-to-deck, import/export of common formats (PDF/PPTX export per vendor materials; slide-count limit per prompt not verified)
  • Custom brand themes shared across a workspace; 100 custom domains on Business (10 on Pro)
  • Gamma Imagine: AI graphics with 100+ templates (TechCrunch, Mar 2026)
  • Integrations with ChatGPT, Claude, Make, Zapier, Atlassian, n8n (TechCrunch); exact connector count (37) not verified
  • Enterprise: admin controls, SCIM, audit logs with SIEM export, guest management, API key/connector governance

Enterprise data handling

Help centre: on Business the training opt-out is locked for the whole workspace; on Pro it is a setting; Enterprise includes locked opt-out. SSO on Business; SCIM, audit logs/SIEM export, sharing and guest controls, and API-key/connector governance on Enterprise. SOC 2 documentation available on request (help centre). Free/Plus individual-tier training default: not verified (third-party aifoxx lists 'Trains on your data: Unknown'). trust.gamma.app opened but rendered no text, so SOC 2 Type II date, 2026 pen test, EU SCCs/US-only processing and subprocessor list are not verified. aifoxx reports no ISO 27001/HIPAA evidence.

Licensing and pricing

Official help centre: Business US$40 per member per month billed annually, 10,000 credits per member per month (Pro 4,000), unused credits roll over up to 2x monthly limit; Enterprise via sales, annual agreement, invoiced billing, custom credits. Gamma pricing page returned HTTP 403 so Free/Plus/Pro/Ultra/Team prices are not verified (a competitor blog, presentations.ai, was opened but its figures were not checked line by line; treat as indicative only).

Integrations

Per TechCrunch: ChatGPT, Claude, Make, Zapier, Atlassian, n8n. Other listed integrations (Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Gong, Microsoft Copilot etc.) not verified.

2025-2026 developments

10 Nov 2025: US$68M Series B led by a16z at US$2.1B valuation, US$100M ARR, 70M users (TechCrunch). 17 Mar 2026: Gamma Imagine launched; company said approaching 100M users. Enterprise plan documented in help centre. SOC 2 Type II date and phishing-abuse report not re-verified.

Best-fit handbook departments

Strongest: Sales, Business Development, Pre-Sales, Marketing, L&D, PMO status decks, Office of the CEO, Investor Relations, Change communications. Weak: BI, Enterprise Architecture, Quality Management, Health & Safety, ESG (packaging only).

Training notes

Outline-first prompting, brand theme setup, credit budgeting, fact and chart verification, public-link hygiene, export fidelity checks. Core 6-8 hours; Advanced 12-16 hours for automation/API users.

Caveats

Consumption credits; plan changes quickly; individual-tier training default and hosting location not verified; pricing page blocked so lower-tier prices unverified.
