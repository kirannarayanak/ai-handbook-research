What it is

What the tool is and where it lives, in plain language.

The AI layer of the Zendesk Suite help desk. Autonomous AI agents resolve customer and employee requests across messaging, email and voice, while Copilot assists human agents. Since the Forethought acquisition the agents can also run on non-Zendesk platforms.

Vendor and product family
Zendesk, Inc. Sold as Zendesk AI on the Zendesk Resolution Platform: AI agents (including Forethought AI Agents by Zendesk), Copilot, Knowledge, QA and Voice.
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

The main things it can do today.

  • AI agents that resolve multi-step workflows across channels and take action in business systems
  • Copilot: reply suggestions and actions for agents
  • Knowledge grounding and intelligent triage that classifies every ticket
  • AI quality assurance scoring human and AI agents
  • Agent Builder for custom agents (early access per earlier research; not re-verified)

Enterprise data handling

What happens to the information you type in: whether the vendor trains on it, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and which security standards it meets.

Verified on the Trust Center: ISO 42001, CSA STAR AI Levels 1 and 2, FedRAMP authorised at Low Impact SaaS (LI-SaaS) level only; HIPAA-enabled account requires the Advanced Security or Advanced Compliance add-on plus a BAA; region selection requires the Data Center Location add-on. SOC 2/ISO 27001 and similar are listed but exact regions, SSO details, and the claim that Zendesk trains generic classification models on Service Data by default while OpenAI features run under zero retention were not re-verified from opened text.

Licensing and pricing

How it is paid for: per user, per usage, or bundled with something you already own.

Verified on the pricing page (annual billing): Support Team $19/agent/mo, Suite Team $55, Suite Professional $115; monthly billing $25/$69/$149. Add-ons per agent/mo annual: $50, $50 and $83 (Copilot, Workforce Engagement and Contact Center per earlier research). Automated resolutions are the AI billing unit: additional committed $1.50, pay-as-you-go $2.00. Included resolutions per plan are described as plan- and agent-count dependent; the specific 5/10 per agent figures were not verified. Suite Enterprise: contact sales.

Integrations

The other systems it connects to, and which handbook departments that matters for.

Native Zendesk channels; Forethought AI Agents by Zendesk work 'within Zendesk and across any platform' (verified); Salesforce and Freshworks named earlier, not re-verified.

2025-2026 developments

What changed recently, including renames and products being retired.

26 Mar 2026: Zendesk completed the Forethought acquisition (announced 11 Mar 2026), verified. 19 May 2026 Relate: Autonomous Service Workforce, AI agents across messaging, email and voice priced on verified resolutions (CMSWire). 'Automated resolution' remains the billing unit on the pricing page.

Training notes

What a learner must be able to do, and how many hours to budget.

Agents: Copilot use, verifying AI drafts, escalation handling, PII awareness (Core 6-8 hours). Admins: knowledge hygiene, triage configuration, resolution-cost monitoring, QA calibration, disabling unwanted AI features (Advanced 12-16 hours).

Caveats

The things most likely to go wrong or surprise a buyer.

Per-resolution billing rises as automation improves; resolution definitions are still evolving; HIPAA and data locality are paid add-ons; FedRAMP only at LI-SaaS level; training-on-Service-Data default not re-verified, so check the current terms.

Jargon on this page

  • AgentAn AI that does not just answer but takes actions: runs code, opens tickets, sends messages, edits systems. Agents need an approval step for anything that cannot be undone.
  • TierThe handbook's training depth: Core (6-10 hours per tool), Advanced (12-20), Specialist (20-40+). A department's tier sets how long to budget.
  • VerifiedVerified means a researcher opened the page and read the claim on it. Not verified means it came from a search snippet or a page that would not load, so treat it with care.
  • RetentionHow long a tool keeps your prompts and outputs. Enterprise plans usually let an administrator set this.
  • CopilotMicrosoft's name for its AI assistants inside Word, Excel, Teams, Power BI, security tools and Azure. One licence covers several of them.
  • SOCSecurity Operations Centre: the team that watches for cyber attacks. SIEM is the software that collects their security logs.
