Qualtrics (Qualtrics International)

Qualtrics AI

AI inside the Qualtrics XM suite. Experience Agents are customer-facing autonomous agents that detect and help resolve issues or dissatisfaction within a survey and make ticket resolution more efficient, alongside text analytics and Insights Explorer.

What it is

AI inside the Qualtrics XM suite. Experience Agents are customer-facing autonomous agents that detect and help resolve issues or dissatisfaction within a survey and make ticket resolution more efficient, alongside text analytics and Insights Explorer.

Vendor and product family
Qualtrics (Qualtrics International). Marketed as Experience Agents plus the XM platform's AI features; 'XM Data & AI' is the platform announced for 2027. 'Qualtrics AI' is a handbook label, not a product name.
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

  • Experience Agents added to existing programs (survey and ticket use cases verified)
  • Insights Explorer, Conversational Feedback and Edge Audiences (announced Oct 2025)
  • Sensitive-data controls to redact or restrict PII collection
  • Role-based access, SSO and MFA

Enterprise data handling

Verified: Qualtrics 'doesn't use your raw customer data to train its AI models' but creates anonymized, aggregated datasets from customer data to train and improve its own features; third-party model vendors are contractually barred from training on customer data. Security page confirms ISO 27001, ISO 42001, FedRAMP High and IL4, HITRUST, SOC 2 Type 2, SSO and MFA. Specific claims on AWS regions, zero retention and survey-agent incompatibility with sensitive-data policies were not re-verified.

Licensing and pricing

The pricing page offers 'Request Pricing' with no list prices; the specific units (per interaction, per employee, per response) were not confirmed. Verified from product terms: an 'Agent Engagement' is an agent's first autonomous action, and up to 500 Agent Engagements per month can be built and tested free within Agent Studio. Support docs state Experience Agents are available only on the new simplified pricing and packaging or the Preview Program, configured with Qualtrics implementation services.

Integrations

Embedded surveys and websites, ticketing within XM. Specific third-party integrations (LangGraph, review sites, AWS) not re-verified.

2025-2026 developments

7 Oct 2025: Experience Agents announcement. 9 Sept 2026: XM Data & AI platform announced for availability in 2027 (verified); the release also confirms the $6.75B Press Ganey Forsta acquisition in May 2026.

Best-fit handbook departments

38 Customer Experience & Service Design (Advanced): voice-of-customer synthesis, journey diagnostics and closed-loop service actions.

38 Customer Experience & Service Design

Training notes

Survey design, validating text-analytics topics, verifying summaries against source responses, agent guardrails and approval workflows, sensitive-data policies, methodological caveats of synthetic audiences. Advanced band, 12-20 hours.

Caveats

No public pricing; Experience Agents are gated to new packaging and paid implementation; product terms state outputs 'may be inaccurate or contain material errors'; anonymized aggregated customer data is used to train Qualtrics models; XM Data & AI does not ship until 2027.
