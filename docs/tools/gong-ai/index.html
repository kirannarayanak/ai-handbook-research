What it is

What the tool is and where it lives, in plain language.

Gong records and transcribes sales calls, meetings and emails, then uses AI to summarise conversations, flag deal risk, coach reps and forecast revenue. It lives in its own web and mobile app and is surfaced inside Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Teams and Zoom; since February 2026 it also exposes MCP servers so Microsoft 365 Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce and HubSpot agents can query Gong data.

Vendor and product family
Gong.io Inc. Gong markets the platform as the "Gong Revenue AI OS" with modules Gong Engage, Gong Forecast, Gong Enable, Gong Agents and Gong Assistant.
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

The main things it can do today.

  • Call and meeting capture, transcription and AI summaries across Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and telephony
  • Gong Assistant: conversational Q&A grounded in recorded customer conversations (launched Feb 2026)
  • Gong Forecast: AI deal-risk scoring and pipeline forecasting
  • Gong Engage: sequences, dialer and AI-drafted follow-ups
  • Gong Enable: AI Call Reviewer scorecards, AI Trainer role-play and Initiative Tracking
  • Account Console and Account Boards for renewal-risk and expansion signals; Gong Agents automate CRM edits and follow-ups

Enterprise data handling

What happens to the information you type in: whether the vendor trains on it, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and which security standards it meets.

Single enterprise contract (no self-serve tier). Gong's trust page states "Your data is never used to train generative AI models" and that customer data is not co-mingled; encryption, MFA and least-privilege access are listed. Default retention is up to three years or the contract duration, whichever is shorter, with custom retention on request and permanent deletion 30 days after termination. The trust page lists SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001:2022, 27017, 27018, 27701, ISO 42001, PCI DSS SAQ-D, CSA STAR and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. HIPAA-related security requirements are listed, but covered-entity status and FedRAMP are not verified. Gong help docs say an admin can choose an EU data centre, whereas a Dec 2025 third-party (competitor Oliv) DPA review claimed US-only storage, so confirm residency contractually. Redaction, deny lists, consent management and SSO details were not re-verified.

Licensing and pricing

How it is paid for: per user, per usage, or bundled with something you already own.

No published list prices. The pricing page confirms per-user licences plus a platform fee based on users supported, with quotes from a sales form by team size. Third-party estimate (Docket, May 2026): roughly US$1,400-3,000 per user per year depending on bundle, platform fee US$5,000-50,000, implementation US$15,000-65,000. Contract-term length (2-3 years) is not verified. Not bundled with any suite.

Integrations

The other systems it connects to, and which handbook departments that matters for.

Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, Microsoft Dynamics 365; Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet; Slack; Snowflake and BI tools; LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, G2, Gainsight, Chili Piper, Zapier; Microsoft 365 Copilot and other MCP clients; partner ecosystem in the Gong Collective (partner count not verified).

2025-2026 developments

What changed recently, including renames and products being retired.

Feb 25 2026 "Mission Andromeda" release: Gong Enable, Gong Assistant, Account Console/Boards and MCP interoperability. Gong's site says it is a Leader again in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Revenue Action Orchestration (earlier Dec 2025 Leader claim not re-verified). VentureBeat reports roughly US$300M ARR and 5,000+ customers. Platform branded "Revenue AI OS" (not a rename of Gong itself).

Training notes

What a learner must be able to do, and how many hours to budget.

Learners must master call-recording consent and regional recording law, tracker and redaction configuration, critical reading of deal warnings and forecast categories, prompting Gong Assistant and checking answers against transcripts, and CRM hygiene. Core 6-8 hours for sellers; Advanced 12-16 hours for RevOps admins (editorial estimates).

Caveats

The things most likely to go wrong or surprise a buyer.

Transcription accuracy can degrade with accents; Docket finds Gong Assistant weak on deeper queries; trackers need maintenance; expensive with multi-year terms; pricing opaque. Claims about the EU data centre disabling dialer features, API bulk export and early-termination penalties were not re-verified.

Jargon on this page

  • AgentAn AI that does not just answer but takes actions: runs code, opens tickets, sends messages, edits systems. Agents need an approval step for anything that cannot be undone.
  • PromptThe instruction or question you give an AI tool. Clear prompts with context and a desired format get better results.
  • TierThe handbook's training depth: Core (6-10 hours per tool), Advanced (12-20), Specialist (20-40+). A department's tier sets how long to budget.
  • VerifiedVerified means a researcher opened the page and read the claim on it. Not verified means it came from a search snippet or a page that would not load, so treat it with care.
  • Data residencyWhere your data is stored and processed. Some tools keep EU or UK data in Europe; some process everything in the US. This matters for privacy law.
  • RetentionHow long a tool keeps your prompts and outputs. Enterprise plans usually let an administrator set this.
  • RAGRetrieval-augmented generation: the AI looks up your documents first and answers from them, which reduces but does not remove errors.
  • MCPModel Context Protocol: a standard way to connect an AI tool to other systems (files, tickets, databases). Powerful, so access should be scoped carefully.
