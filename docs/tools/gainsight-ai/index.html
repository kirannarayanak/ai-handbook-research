What it is

AI layer of the Gainsight CS customer-success platform. Staircase AI reads emails, meetings, tickets, chats and Slack to score sentiment, churn risk and expansion; Atlas pairs AI renewal agents with human Renewal Agent Managers to run long-tail renewals as a service. (Product details other than Atlas and the pricing change could not be re-verified: gainsight.com returned HTTP 403 to every fetch.)

Vendor and product family
Gainsight, Inc. Sold as Staircase AI (the Insight Agent inside Gainsight CS), Atlas (AI-native services) and the Agentic Stack (Agent Studio, MCP, CLI); 'Gainsight AI' is a handbook label, not a product name.
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

  • Churn-risk, sentiment and stakeholder-disengagement alerts from customer conversations (vendor claim, not re-verified)
  • Health scorecards, playbooks, Customer 360 and renewal/expansion forecasting in Gainsight CS (not re-verified)
  • Atlas managed renewals: AI agents plus Renewal Agent Managers who step in when judgement is needed (verified via CFOtech)
  • Agent Studio (beta) and Agentic Stack with MCP and CLI (not re-verified)
  • Customers can connect their own agents through MCP at no added charge (verified via CFOtech)

Enterprise data handling

Not verified: Gainsight's security and AI data-security pages returned HTTP 403 and could not be opened. The earlier claims (customer data not used to train LLMs, AI features off by default, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001/42001, HIPAA, EU hosting option for Staircase, no FedRAMP) must be confirmed on gainsight.com/security before use.

Licensing and pricing

Per CFOtech (22 Oct 2026): Gainsight introduced value-based pricing for Gainsight CS, replacing seat-based pricing with a model tied to ARR and number of customers managed, available next year (2027), including Staircase AI and with no charge for MCP access. Atlas manages over $75M customer ARR and 7,000+ agentic interactions (Manila Times/GlobeNewswire). Current plan names (Essentials/Enterprise, user counts) and 'Request Pricing' not verified (pricing page 403).

Integrations

Not verified from opened pages (Salesforce, Slack, Zoom, Gong, HubSpot, MCP clients were claimed earlier). MCP access is confirmed by CFOtech.

2025-2026 developments

Oct 2026 (verified): value-based pricing announced; Atlas over $75M ARR managed, 7,000+ agentic interactions; Atlas introduced in May 2026 as AI-native services business per CFOtech. Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader (third year), May 2026 Agentic Stack details and Salesforce partnership: not re-verified (pages 403).

Best-fit handbook departments

36 Customer Success (Advanced): direct fit for customer health, renewal-risk detection and success plans.

36 Customer Success

Training notes

Health-score design, reading risk signals without over-reacting, playbooks, human review of agent-drafted renewal outreach, consent and privacy for mailbox and meeting ingestion, deliberately enabling AI features, basic MCP use. Advanced band, 12-16 hours.

Caveats

Vendor pages blocked verification; no confirmed public list pricing and the model changes in 2027; Atlas is an outsourced service, not just software; mailbox and meeting ingestion needs privacy and works-council review; risk and sentiment scores are probabilistic; data-handling claims unverified.
