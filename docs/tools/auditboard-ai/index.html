Learning path and certifications

AuditBoard is now Optro. Optro Academy offers role-based learning paths (about 6 to 7+ hours each, CPE eligible) with live training sessions, a certification exam and a badge. Start with the product 101 course, then the team member or administrator path, then the exam.

What it is

Cloud GRC platform for internal audit, SOX, risk, infosec and compliance with embedded AI agents (Optro AI, Autonomous Testing, Agentic Compliance/TPRM, AI Governance). Web platform with an MCP server for external assistants (per vendor/news claims, not re-verified here).

Vendor and product family
Optro (formerly AuditBoard; owned by Hg since 2024). RENAME: 'AuditBoard' is outdated; the company rebranded as Optro in March 2026 and its AI is marketed as 'Optro AI'.
Handbook category
Legal, audit & GRC

Key capabilities

  • Autonomous Testing of control evidence producing cited, audit-ready workpapers (vendor claim)
  • Audit planning and drafting assistance (audit descriptions, summaries, action plans)
  • Controls Management, OpsAudit, CrossComply, RiskOversight, TPRM and AI Governance modules (product names per Optro pricing page)
  • GRC Intelligence Platform with agents operating inside team-defined guardrails; every agent action logged and cited (Help Net Security, 15 Oct 2026)

Enterprise data handling

Optro AI page: 'Optro does not use customer data to train Optro AI or models'; third-party integrations such as Microsoft Azure are used; customers retain ownership of inputs and outputs. Trust & Security page: AWS hosting meeting FedRAMP Moderate impact requirements (hosting, not product authorisation), TLS 1.2/1.3, AES-256, AWS KMS, single-tenant architecture, NIST-compliant deletion; assessed against frameworks including ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, SOC 2, CSA STAR and HIPAA. Not verified: SOC 1 report, 'HIPAA Type 1' report, TX-RAMP, per-customer opt-in for AI, data-residency regions.

Licensing and pricing

No published prices; Optro pricing page cites flexible plans, unlimited stakeholder licences and 'no hidden fees'. ERP Research (2026) reports a median ~$46,000/year with a $21k-$150k range, driven by modules taken; Techno-Pulse (Jul 2026) cites $30k-$75k for 5-15 auditors scaling to $150k+. Per-core-user pricing and whether AI is a paid add-on are not verified.

Integrations

Per Optro materials (not individually re-verified): ticketing, collaboration, ERP, cloud and HR system connectors, REST/SCIM APIs; MCP server for external AI assistants.

2025-2026 developments

Mar 2026: AuditBoard renamed Optro (Optro blog 'AuditBoard is now Optro'). 15 Oct 2026: GRC Intelligence Platform announced with agents for audit, compliance, TPRM and AI governance. Oct 2025 FairNow and May 2026 Midship acquisitions, April 2026 MCP server, SOXHUB renamed Controls Management and the Forrester Wave placement are cited in the original research but were not re-verified.

Best-fit handbook departments

08 Internal Audit: strong fit (OpsAudit plus Autonomous Testing and Controls Management for SOX). It also serves enterprise risk, compliance and infosec teams not in the handbook mapping.

Training notes

Specialist band, 24-32 hours: tenant, RBAC and SSO setup; AI guardrail configuration aligned to risk appetite; evidence quality and sampling vs full-population analytics; reviewing and approving agent-generated workpapers; audit-log and traceability review; MCP governance; updating vendor-risk files for the Optro name.

Caveats

Autonomous-testing and SOX automation percentage claims are vendor claims without independent validation. Module-based licensing can drive cost creep. The rebrand may leave stale documentation, review-site listings and certificates; confirm DPAs carry current no-training terms. FedRAMP applies to hosting, not the product.
