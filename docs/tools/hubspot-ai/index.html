What it is

AI embedded throughout HubSpot's customer platform (Marketing, Sales, Service, Content and Revenue Hubs). Breeze Assistant is an in-app chat assistant; Agent Hub is the central home for building and managing AI agents; prebuilt agents include Customer and Prospecting agents. Connectors to ChatGPT and other assistants exist (ChatGPT connector article cited; others not re-verified).

Vendor and product family
HubSpot, Inc. Marketed as Breeze Assistant, Agent Hub, Agent Builder and Smart CRM. Whether the "Breeze" umbrella brand is being retired is not verified (the Fall 2026 Spotlight page still uses "Breeze Assistant").
Handbook category
Sales, marketing & customer

Key capabilities

  • Prospecting Agent monitors buying signals and drafts outreach (US$1 per lead recommended for outreach)
  • Customer Agent resolves customer enquiries (US$0.50 per resolved conversation; 65% resolution claim not re-verified)
  • Breeze Assistant drafts emails and content, summarises records and preps meetings
  • Campaign, Content and Nurture agents (listed in Spotlight/marketing pages; detail not re-verified)
  • Smart CRM enrichment and buyer intent plus Data Agent

Enterprise data handling

The HubSpot KB confirms the "AI Model Training" toggle is on by default, only Super Admins can turn it off, and accounts with Sensitive Data turned on are automatically opted out. HubSpot's AI trust page says AI service providers are contractually required not to train on HubSpot customer data. Trust Center lists SOC 1 Type II, HIPAA and 2026 SOC 2 reports. Specific claims about Model Zero Data Retention, PII redaction, EU data centre, SSO, and absence of FedRAMP/ISO 27001 were not re-verified.

Licensing and pricing

Not verified: HubSpot's Sales Hub and Marketing Hub pricing pages returned no readable text, so seat prices, onboarding fees and credit allowances previously listed are removed. Verified: from 14 Apr 2026 Customer Agent costs US$0.50 per resolved conversation (was US$1.00 per conversation) and Prospecting Agent US$1 per lead recommended for outreach, both for Pro and Enterprise. Confirm current tiers with HubSpot sales.

Integrations

Native Smart CRM; ChatGPT connector; other connectors, HubSpot MCP Server, Gong/G2/Asana connectors and Sales Navigator sync not re-verified.

2025-2026 developments

14 Apr 2026: outcome-based pricing for Customer and Prospecting agents. Fall 2026 Spotlight (UNBOUND) exists and centres on "Growth Context"; AEO (Beta) is listed in the product menu. Other dated claims (Sep 2025 rename, Jul 2026 beta, SOC 1 in June 2026) not re-verified beyond the Trust Center listing SOC 1 Type II.

Best-fit handbook departments

29 Sales - SMB & Inside Sales (Core): good fit (Prospecting Agent, Assistant, Sales Hub sequences). 32 Digital & Performance Marketing (Advanced): useful for campaign planning, nurture and reporting, but ad-auction optimisation is shallower than native Google or Meta tooling.

Listed for these departments in the handbook

Training notes

Master agent monitoring in Agent Hub, AI access and model-training settings, writing the knowledge content agents depend on, editing generic drafts, and connector permission scoping. Core 6-10 hours for sales users; Advanced 12-16 hours for marketing ops and admins (editorial estimates).

Caveats

Agent quality depends on CRM data completeness; default opt-in to HubSpot model training surprises buyers; many agents are beta and change fast; frequent renames make internal documentation stale. Credit non-rollover and mandatory onboarding fees are not verified.
