What it is

SAP's generative-AI copilot, embedded in SAP cloud applications (S/4HANA Cloud, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur, Fieldglass and others) and Microsoft Teams; Joule Studio builds custom agents. Per SAP's May 2026 Sapphire release, Joule sits within the SAP Business AI Platform and Autonomous Suite.

Vendor and product family
SAP SE; Joule is the assistant and agent layer of SAP Business AI.
Handbook category
Enterprise business platforms

Key capabilities

  • Navigational, informational, transactional and analytical capability types
  • Joule Skills across finance, sourcing and procurement, supply chain, HR and expense/travel
  • 50+ domain Joule Assistants (SAP press release, 12 May 2026); '200+ agents' figure not re-verified
  • Joule Studio for no-code to pro-code agent building
  • Joule Work/Desktop and SAP AI Agent Hub details not re-verified

Enterprise data handling

SAP cites ISO 42001 (independent registry: scope includes Joule, AI Core, AI Launchpad). SAP's Teams-app attestation (Microsoft Learn, May 2025) lists SOC and ISO attestations, self-reported. Activation requires adding SKU 8019544 (0 EUR) or AI Units SKU 8019164 (confirmed on SAP Learning). Explicit 'no training on customer data' commitments were not found on pages opened. SAP's own trust pages were not opened.

Licensing and pricing

Confirmed on SAP Learning: Joule Base is included at no additional cost in qualifying SAP cloud subscriptions (activation via SKU 8019544, 0 EUR); Premium AI today mainly per-user-per-month packages (Finance, HCM, Supply Chain, Spend, Customer Experience), moving during Q3 2026 to AI Unit consumption with every agent action consuming 0.02 AI Units in 2026. Per Redress (third party): minimum purchase 100 AI Units, expiry after 12 months, Joule for Consultants 35 AI Units per user per month; unit price only in the order form. 'Free runtime for custom Studio agents through end-2026' is not verified. Not bundled with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.

Integrations

S/4HANA Cloud, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur, Fieldglass, BTP; Microsoft Teams; model partners including Anthropic, AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, NVIDIA and Palantir named in the SAP press release; MCP/A2A support not re-verified.

2025-2026 developments

12 May 2026 Sapphire: SAP Business AI Platform, Autonomous Suite, 50+ Joule Assistants, Joule Studio (SAP News). Oct 2026 general availability of Joule Work/Desktop is reported by SiliconANGLE (not re-opened). Q3 2026 AI Unit consumption model for agents (SAP Learning).

Best-fit handbook departments

All five fit: 46 Enterprise Apps & ERP (S/4HANA, BTP, Joule Studio), 25 Procurement (Ariba and Fieldglass agents), 60 Supply Chain, 21 Accounting (finance skills), 24 AP/AR (finance transactional skills; Document AI is metered). Weakest is 24 where invoicing runs outside SAP cloud editions.

Training notes

Learners must know their edition and which skills are activated, phrase transactional versus informational requests, verify agent output against SAP authorisations, and monitor AI Unit use (scheduled agents multiply consumption). Advanced (21, 24, 25, 60): 12-20 h; Specialist (46): 20-40 h covering SAP for Me activation, Joule Studio, unit monitoring and Teams integration.

Caveats

No Joule on classic on-premise S/4HANA, ECC, Business One or self-hosted editions (not re-verified); the 2026 shift from per-user packages to per-action metering creates mid-contract uncertainty; units expire annually (Redress); much of the agent portfolio is roadmap; sap.com product and trust pages were not verified; the Teams attestation is self-reported and dated May 2025.
