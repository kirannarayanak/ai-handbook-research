What it is

Source-grounded research and writing tool: users upload documents and sources and receive cited answers plus generated study and briefing artefacts. Enterprise edition runs inside a Google Cloud project.

Vendor and product family
Google. Rename: NotebookLM became Gemini Notebook on 16 Jul 2026 (verified); NotebookLM Enterprise is now Gemini Notebook Enterprise (Google Cloud docs).
Handbook category
Google ecosystem

Key capabilities

  • Cited Q&A over uploaded sources
  • Audio and Video Overviews, mind maps, reports, quizzes and similar outputs (per Google subscription pages; full list not verified)
  • Per-notebook source and query limits vary by plan (support page shows tiers of 100/300/500 per notebook); the earlier 'free 50 sources' figure is not verified

Enterprise data handling

Consumer: content not used to directly train foundation models unless the user gives feedback; feedback is human-reviewed and retained up to 3 years, disconnected from the account. Workspace/Education: uploads, queries and responses are not human-reviewed (even with feedback) and not used to train models. Google states Gemini Notebook does not support ISO/SOC/FedRAMP and is not covered by the HIPAA BAA. Gemini Notebook Enterprise supports data residency, CMEK (US/EU multi-region), VPC-SC and Access Transparency per Google Cloud docs.

Licensing and pricing

Free with a Google account; higher limits via Google AI Plus $4.99, Pro $19.99, Ultra $99.99-$199.99/month (verified). Included in Workspace plans (Workspace pricing page); the $14 Business Standard price is not verified. Enterprise edition pricing: not verified; contact Google.

Integrations

Google Drive, Docs and Slides, URLs; Office file support and export formats: not verified.

2025-2026 developments

16 Jul 2026 rename to Gemini Notebook, with automatic redirects (verified). Other dated items (8 Jun, 1 Aug, 14 Sep, 22 Oct 2026): not re-verified.

Best-fit handbook departments

13 L&D (Core); 33 Market Research, 04 Board Secretariat and 39 Product Management for document search. Specialist 03, 06, 07 only on Gemini Notebook Enterprise because the consumer/Workspace edition lacks attestations.

Training notes

Curating and trusting sources, reading citations, sharing scopes, choosing the right edition, reviewing generated overviews for oversimplification. Core 6-8 h; Specialist 20 h including governance set-up.

Caveats

Rename leaves mixed documentation; compliance gap outside the Enterprise edition; per-notebook limits; Enterprise pricing unpublished.
