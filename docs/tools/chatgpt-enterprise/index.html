What it is

OpenAI's managed ChatGPT plan for organisations: ChatGPT apps plus an admin console. Feature list in the earlier draft (Company Knowledge, workspace agents, ChatGPT Work, Codex seats) could not be verified because all openai.com and help.openai.com pages returned HTTP 403.

Vendor and product family
OpenAI. Family: ChatGPT Free/Go/Plus/Pro (consumer), ChatGPT Business (formerly Team), ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu; Codex; API platform.
Handbook category
General AI assistant

Key capabilities

  • Connected-app knowledge search, custom GPTs, Projects, Deep Research, data analysis, Canvas, memory (all not verified)
  • Agents and Codex coding agent (not verified)

Enterprise data handling

Not verified: every OpenAI privacy, security and residency page failed to open (HTTP 403). The earlier claims (no training by default, admin retention, SOC 2 Type 2, ISO certifications, residency regions, BAA) should be re-checked against OpenAI's pages before publication.

Licensing and pricing

Enterprise price is unpublished (contact sales, annual). CloudZero (opened) reports about $60/user/month (range $45-75), 150-seat minimum, annual prepayment, with newer eligible agreements adding token-based billing; OpenAI has not confirmed these. ChatGPT Business prices ($25/$20 Standard; $125/$100 Premium): not verified.

Integrations

Not verified (OpenAI pages inaccessible).

2025-2026 developments

Not verified. The model-release dates (GPT-5.5 Instant, GPT-6, GPT-6.1 Sol), Aug 2026 Premium seats and Feb 2026 Frontier platform from the earlier draft could not be re-opened; the Team-to-Business rename is also unconfirmed here, though CloudZero refers to Business starting at two seats.

Best-fit handbook departments

Core 01, 11, 13, 17, 31, 33, 34, 39, 50 and Advanced 02, 20, 27, 35, 38, 40, 52, 57, 58 fit well; 41/42 via Codex. Weaker: 03 M&A, 22 Treasury, 53/59 (judgement, not verified against vendor data).

Training notes

Model and reasoning picker, Projects and connector permissions, agent-mode risks and approvals, verifying outputs, data-residency awareness, governance of GPT publishing. Core 6-10 h; Advanced 12-18 h; Specialist 20-30 h.

Caveats

Most vendor claims unverified because OpenAI sites block automated access; Enterprise price is negotiated; agents act on your systems and need governance; consumer ChatGPT has different defaults. Verify security, residency and pricing directly with OpenAI.
